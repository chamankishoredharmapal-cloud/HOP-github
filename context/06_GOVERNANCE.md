# 06 Governance

Status: APPROVED
Version: v1.0
State: Operating Constitution

> This document defines how the House decides. It is the operating constitution that ensures every decision is made with institutional discipline, protecting the strategy from the pressure of daily operations.

## 1. Decision-Making Philosophy

Decisions at House of Padmavati are not made by consensus or convenience; they are governed by institutional conviction. If a decision cannot be traced back to a principle established in the Foundational Authority (`01_BRAND_STRATEGY.md`), the decision is rejected. Speed is subordinated to precision.

## 2. Approval Authority

- **Founder:** Sole authority for `00_HOP_BRAND_CONTEXT.md` (as the source of beliefs), `01_BRAND_STRATEGY.md`, `06_GOVERNANCE.md`, and final approval of all Domain Languages (`02`, `03`, `04`, `05`), the Glossary (`08`), and strategic decisions in `07`.
- **Domain Leads (Visual, Product, Brand Strategist):** Primary authors and custodians of their respective documents, responsible for ensuring alignment with `01` before presenting for Founder approval.

## 3. Decision Escalation Rules

- **Operational Decisions:** May be made by domain leads provided they strictly adhere to the approved domain language document.
- **Strategic Decisions:** Any decision that introduces a new principle, modifies an existing one, or addresses an open question must be escalated to the Founder for approval and recorded in `07_DECISIONS_LOG.md`.

## 4. Brand Consistency Review and Quality Gates

Before any output (a product, an image, a message, an experience) is released, it must pass a quality gate:
- **Derivation Rule:** Can this output be justified by an upstream document?
- **Replaceability Test:** Could another brand produce this exact output?
- If the answer to the first is 'No' or the second is 'Yes', the output fails the gate and cannot proceed.

## 5. Incorporating New Strategic Discoveries

- Raw discoveries are immediately appended to `00_HOP_BRAND_CONTEXT.md`.
- If a discovery challenges existing strategy, it is surfaced as an open question in `09_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.
- Upon resolution by the Founder, the decision is logged in `07_DECISIONS_LOG.md`, and the relevant upstream documents (`01–05`) are updated through a new version.

## 6. Conflict Resolution

- **The Iron Rule of Hierarchy:** If a conflict exists between any two documents, the upstream document always wins.
- The downstream document must be revised to align with the upstream authority. The resolution is recorded in `07_DECISIONS_LOG.md`.

## 7. Document Update Process

1. Identify the need for revision (strategic evolution or error correction).
2. Draft the revision.
3. Verify alignment with upstream documents (Derivation Rule).
4. Obtain Founder approval (for major/minor versions).
5. Publish the new version.
6. Update `10_CHANGELOG.md` with the version bump and rationale.
7. Update `07_DECISIONS_LOG.md` if the change reflects a strategic decision.

## 8. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Founder:** Ultimate arbiter of the institution's standards and strategy.
- **Brand Strategist:** Custodian of the Knowledge Architecture. Responsible for maintaining the integrity of the system, tracking `07`, `08`, `09`, and `10`, and ensuring all domain languages adhere to `01`.
- **Domain Leads:** Responsible for executing within the boundaries of their respective domain languages.

## 9. Maintenance of Living Documents

- **07 Decisions Log:** Updated by the Brand Strategist immediately upon Founder approval of any strategic decision. Never retroactively edited.
- **10 Changelog:** Updated by the Brand Strategist immediately upon any version revision of a document in the `/context` architecture.
