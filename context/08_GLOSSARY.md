# 08 Glossary

> The institutional vocabulary of House of Padmavati. Contains only officially approved HOP terminology and definitions.

## Foundational Terms

- **The House**: The preferred term for HOP, emphasizing its nature as a long-term institution rather than a commercial brand or shop.
- **Institution**: An entity built for permanence, guided by enduring principles rather than quarterly outcomes.
- **Living Craftsmanship**: The accumulated human intelligence embedded within artisanship, treated as an evolving mastery rather than historical nostalgia.
- **Discernment**: The ability to judge well; the core quality defining the HOP customer, prioritizing meaning and quiet elegance over display and spectacle.
