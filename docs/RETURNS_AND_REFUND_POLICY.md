# Returns & Refund Policy

**House of Padmavati**

*Last updated: July 2026*

---

## 1. Introduction

This Returns & Refund Policy describes the conditions under which House of Padmavati accepts returns, processes exchanges, and issues refunds. By placing an order, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

House of Padmavati is a sole proprietorship based in Karnataka, India.

**Contact Email:** houseofpadmavati@gmail.com

**Contact Phone:** 7975335312

## 2. Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of delivery. Requests made after 7 days will not be accepted.

## 3. Accepted Return Reasons

We accept returns for the following reasons only:

- Wrong product delivered.
- Product damaged during shipping.
- Manufacturing defect.
- Incorrect measurements, only if stitching or customisation was performed by House of Padmavati.

## 4. Non-Acceptable Return Reasons

We do not accept returns for the following reasons:

- Change of mind.
- Did not like the colour or design.
- Ordered by mistake.

## 5. Non-Returnable Products

The following products are not eligible for return under any circumstances:

- Customised products.
- Altered products.
- Worn products.
- Washed products.
- Products without original tags attached.
- Gift cards.
- Clearance sale products.

## 6. Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, the product must:

- Be unworn, unwashed, and unaltered.
- Have all original tags attached.
- Be returned in its original packaging.

We reserve the right to reject returns that do not meet these conditions.

## 7. How to Initiate a Return

To initiate a return, contact us at houseofpadmavati@gmail.com with your order number and reason for return. We will guide you through the process.

## 8. Return Shipping

### 8.1 Return Shipping Paid by House of Padmavati

We bear the return shipping cost for the following reasons:

- Wrong product delivered.
- Product damaged during shipping.
- Manufacturing defect.

### 8.2 Return Shipping Paid by Customer

The customer bears the return shipping cost for voluntary exchanges.

## 9. Exchange Policy

### 9.1 Eligibility

One exchange is permitted per order. The exchange process follows the same conditions as returns.

### 9.2 Exchange Products

You may exchange for a different product. If the exchanged product has a different price, the difference will be payable by you or refundable to you, as applicable.

### 9.3 Exchange Shipping

The customer pays shipping for voluntary exchanges.

## 10. Refund Process

### 10.1 Refund Method

Refunds are issued via bank transfer. Refunds are not issued back to the original payment method.

### 10.2 Refund Timeline

Once we receive the returned product, it undergoes inspection. Please allow 5–7 business days after inspection for the refund to be processed.

### 10.3 Inspection

All returned products are inspected upon receipt. If the product does not meet the return conditions outlined in Section 6, we reserve the right to deny the refund and arrange for the product to be returned to you at your cost.

## 11. Order Cancellation

### 11.1 Before Dispatch

Orders may be cancelled before dispatch at no charge.

### 11.2 After Dispatch

Orders cannot be cancelled after dispatch.

### 11.3 How to Cancel

To cancel an order, contact us at houseofpadmavati@gmail.com with your order number.
