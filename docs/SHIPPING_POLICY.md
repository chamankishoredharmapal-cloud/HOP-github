## 1. Introduction

This Shipping Policy describes how House of Padmavati prepares, dispatches, and delivers your orders. By placing an order, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

## 2. Shipping Destinations

We ship worldwide.

## 3. Shipping Charges

### 3.1 Free Shipping

Free shipping is available for:

- First orders.
- Orders with a total value of ₹2,499 or more.

### 3.2 Standard Shipping Charge

Orders below ₹2,499 that are not first orders are charged a flat shipping fee of ₹99.

## 4. Dispatch Time

Orders are dispatched within 1 business day of order confirmation.

Some handcrafted products may require additional preparation time. You will be notified if your order falls into this category.

## 5. Delivery Timelines

### 5.1 India Delivery

Delivery within India typically takes 3–5 business days from the date of dispatch.

### 5.2 International Delivery

International delivery typically takes 7–8 business days from the date of dispatch, depending on customs clearance in the destination country.

Please note that delivery timelines are estimates and not guaranteed.

## 6. Courier Partners

We use the following courier partners for shipments:

- Delhivery
- Blue Dart
- DTDC
- DHL

## 7. Delivery Requirements

### 7.1 Signature on Delivery

High-value orders require a signature upon delivery. Please ensure someone is available to receive and sign for the package.

### 7.2 PO Box Restriction

We do not deliver to PO Box addresses. A physical delivery address is required for all shipments.

## 8. Insurance

All shipments are fully insured against loss or damage until delivery is confirmed.

## 9. International Customs and Duties

International customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, and local fees imposed by the destination country. These charges are not included in the checkout price. Please check with your local customs office for applicable charges before placing an order.

## 10. Failed Delivery

If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful, the courier will make additional attempts. If delivery continues to fail, the parcel will be returned to House of Padmavati.

We will contact you to arrange re-delivery or process a refund. Additional shipping charges may apply for re-delivery.

## 11. Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can track your shipment through the courier partner's website.

## 12. Shipping Address Accuracy

You are responsible for providing a correct and complete shipping address. We are not liable for delays or losses resulting from incorrect address information provided by you.
