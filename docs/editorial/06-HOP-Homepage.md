# 06 HOP Homepage Editorial Specification

**Status:** Production  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

## 1. Purpose

The homepage is the front door to the House. It exists to establish immediately that House of Padmavati is not an online store—it is a digital fashion house. 

*   **Primary Emotion:** Calm (referencing the *Arrival* stage in [07-emotional-architecture/emotional-journey.md](file:///e:/HOP/docs/editorial/07-emotional-architecture/emotional-journey.md)).
*   **The Editorial Job:** Create silence in a world of noise. Before the woman considers a saree, she must feel relief. The homepage filters out urgency, discounts, and marketing frenzy, offering a quiet room where cloth and craft take precedence.

## 2. Narrative

The homepage tells a story through the scroll. Each section reveals a new layer of the House, transitioning the woman through deliberate emotional states. 

*   **Arrival (Silence):** The hero section asks nothing of the reader. It simply exists.
*   **Curiosity (Collection Names):** The collections are presented as invitations, not categories. 
*   **Wonder (Craft):** We reveal the human scale of the weaving process.
*   **Trust (Testimonials):** We let real women speak for the cloth.
*   **Belonging (Journal):** We invite her to read, proving depth beyond commerce.

## 3. Reading Flow

The eye must move slowly. We are training the reader to slow down.

1.  **First Glance (0% scroll):** Only the silent video and a single, restrained tagline. A moment of pause.
2.  **First Scroll (25% scroll):** The collections appear sequentially. The reading rhythm is staccato but fluid—name, number, short premise.
3.  **Mid-Scroll (50% scroll):** The pace slows for the Craft section. Text becomes slightly denser, anchoring the eye to specific numbers and techniques. 
4.  **Deep Scroll (75% scroll):** Trust sections (Heirlooms, Journal) offer places to linger.
5.  **Footer (100% scroll):** Quiet closure. A gentle exit or a deliberate choice to navigate further.

## 4. Section Goals

### a. Hero Section
*   **Purpose:** Create the feeling of entering a quiet room.
*   **Content:** Silent video of moving cloth, a tagline, navigation menu. No "Shop Now" call to action.
*   **Emotional Objective:** Relief, calm. 
*   **Copy Principles:** Maximum restraint, minimum words. Let the negative space do the heavy lifting. (See [01-HOP-Voice-Bible.md](file:///e:/HOP/docs/editorial/01-HOP-Voice-Bible.md)).

### b. Collection Stage
*   **Purpose:** Introduce the five collections as an invitation to explore.
*   **Content:** Collection name, number (01–05), a single-line tagline, and a silent video for each.
*   **Collections:** 01 Kalyani (Wedding Silk), 02 Viara (Reception Silk), 03 Arya (Working Silk), 04 Padma, 05 Spandana.
*   **Emotional Objective:** Curiosity, gentle fascination.
*   **Copy Principles:** Names before explanations. Hint at the purpose, but do not explain it fully. No marketing jargon. 

### c. Craft Section
*   **Purpose:** Create wonder through specific craft details.
*   **Content:** Headline, supporting text, and a definitive craft claim (e.g., hours per drape).
*   **Emotional Objective:** Reverence, admiration.
*   **Copy Principles:** Sensory density 5/5. Specific, human-scale numbers. We talk about the weaver's hands, the weight of the silk, the sound of the loom.

### d. Heirlooms / Testimonials
*   **Purpose:** Build trust through real voices.
*   **Emotional Objective:** Trust, belonging.
*   **Copy Principles:** Real quotes only. Never fabricate praise. Select quotes that speak to the emotional weight of the saree, not just its appearance. 

### e. Journal Preview
*   **Purpose:** Signal that HOP has depth beyond commerce. We are custodians of a culture.
*   **Emotional Objective:** Contemplation.
*   **Copy Principles:** Article titles should intrigue, not sell. They should read like essays, not blog posts.

### f. Footer
*   **Purpose:** Quiet closure.
*   **Content:** The foundational House tagline, newsletter signup, navigation links, and legal requirements.
*   **Newsletter Label:** "Your email, gently kept."

## 5. Content Hierarchy

1.  **Essential:** The Hero silent video and tagline.
2.  **Essential:** The five collections (names and numbers).
3.  **Important:** The Craft section detailing the physical reality of the weave.
4.  **Supporting:** Heirlooms and Journal entries (rotates based on editorial calendar).
5.  **Functional:** Footer navigation. 

## 6. Copy Principles

*   **Restraint is Key:** The homepage uses fewer words than any other page. Every word must justify its existence. 
*   **No Urgency:** Never use words like "Now," "Hurry," or "Limited." (Review [12-HOP-Copy-Review-Checklist.md](file:///e:/HOP/docs/editorial/12-HOP-Copy-Review-Checklist.md) for forbidden words).
*   **Nouns and Verbs:** Rely on strong, tactile nouns (loom, thread, drape) and gentle verbs (invite, welcome, fold). 
*   **The Oxford Comma:** Always required.
*   **Punctuation:** Em dashes with spaces on both sides. Curly quotes. No exclamation marks.

## 7. Approved Final Copy

This is the production copy ready for implementation, adhering to the [04-HOP-Content-Architecture.md](file:///e:/HOP/docs/editorial/04-HOP-Content-Architecture.md) framework.

**Hero Section**
*   **Tagline:** A house, not a shop.
*   **Subtext:** Welcome to House of Padmavati. 
*   **Rationale:** Instantly reframes the visitor's expectation from a transactional ecommerce experience to an editorial, immersive one.

**Collection Stage**
*   **01 Kalyani:** The wedding silk. Woven for the day that changes everything.
*   **02 Viara:** The reception silk. Lightness, movement, and quiet celebration.
*   **03 Arya:** The working silk. Authority woven into every yard.
*   **04 Padma:** The everyday silk. Softness for the spaces in between.
*   **05 Spandana:** The signature silk. Our heritage, held in a single drape.
*   **Rationale:** Positions the collections around the woman's life moments rather than fabric types. 

**Craft Section**
*   **Headline:** The hands that hold the thread.
*   **Body Copy:** We do not believe in the rush of the modern loom. A House of Padmavati saree requires sixty hours of deliberate, unhurried attention. You will feel the quietness in the weight of the silk.
*   **Rationale:** Uses sensory language ("weight of the silk") and specific numbers ("sixty hours") to ground the ethereal claims in physical reality.

**Heirlooms Section**
*   **Heading:** Worn and remembered.
*   **Quote Example:** "I wore it, and I felt my grandmother standing right behind me." — Ananya V.
*   **Rationale:** Shifts the focus from the product to the emotional legacy of the wearer.

**Journal Section**
*   **Heading:** Notes from the House.
*   **Article Title Example:** The Geometry of the Border.
*   **Rationale:** Frames the journal as an archive of thought, not a content marketing tool.

**Footer**
*   **House Tagline:** To the woman who wove my world.
*   **Newsletter Title:** Letters from the House.
*   **Newsletter Input Placeholder:** Your email, gently kept.
*   **Rationale:** Ends the page with warmth, respect, and a promise of privacy ("gently kept").

## 8. Rejected Alternatives

To ensure future writers understand the editorial boundaries, here are rejected options and the reasoning behind them:

*   **Rejected Hero:** "Experience the finest handwoven luxury."
    *   *Reason:* Uses forbidden words ("luxury," "finest"). It sounds like generic marketing.
*   **Rejected Collection Copy:** "Shop the Kalyani Wedding Collection."
    *   *Reason:* Uses "Shop." We invite exploration; we do not command transactions. 
*   **Rejected Craft Copy:** "Our weavers empower themselves by making beautiful sarees."
    *   *Reason:* Uses "empower" and "beautiful." It is patronising and generic. We name the weaver; we do not use them as props for our nobility.
*   **Rejected Footer Newsletter:** "Sign up for 10% off your first order."
    *   *Reason:* The House never discounts. We do not bribe women to hear from us. 

## 9. Future Scalability

The homepage is designed to be structurally static but contextually fluid. 

*   **What Must Not Change:** The Hero sequence (silence first), the presence of all five core collections, and the tone of restraint. 
*   **What Can Change:** 
    *   The background silent video in the Hero section can rotate seasonally (e.g., focusing on a specific weaving technique during monsoon, or a finished drape during wedding season). 
    *   The Journal and Heirlooms sections are dynamic, pulling the most recent, relevant entries to ensure the page feels alive but never loud.
    *   If a new collection is introduced, it must follow the numeric structure (06) and the naming convention (a woman's name, not a descriptive label).
