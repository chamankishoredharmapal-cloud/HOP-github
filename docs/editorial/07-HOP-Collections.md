# HOP Collections: Editorial System

**Status:** Production
**Last Updated:** July 2026
**Related Documents:** [Voice Bible](../02-brand-architecture/voice-bible.md), [Content Architecture](04-HOP-Content-Architecture.md), [Product Pages](08-HOP-Product-Pages.md)

## 1. Collection Philosophy

At House of Padmavati, a collection is never a mere assortment of products or a seasonal dump. It is an editorial experience, a curated narrative bound by technique, intention, and mood. 

We name our collections after women—Kalyani, Viara, Arya, Padma, Spandana. Each name is a dedication, not a descriptive label. It grounds the cloth in the real, reminding us that every saree is woven for a woman, meant to be lived in, and destined to outlive its first wearer. The collection page itself must feel like walking into a quiet room where the cloth is the only focus.

## 2. Naming

### Current Collections
- **Kalyani:** The auspicious one. Rooted in heritage, ceremony, and the weight of tradition.
- **Viara:** The dawn. Soft, quiet presence, evening light, and sheer textures.
- **Arya:** The noble rhythm. Grounded, structured, made for daily movement and professional cadence.
- **Padma:** The lotus. Geometric, precise, fine lines that speak to architectural intent.
- **Spandana:** The vibration. Contemporary expressions, evolving the handloom vocabulary.

### Naming Rules for Future Collections
- Must be a woman's name of Indian origin.
- Must carry a distinct emotional or character-driven meaning.
- Must never be a generic or descriptive word (e.g., never "The Wedding Edit" or "Summer Silks").

### Approval Process
All new collection names require approval from the Brand Director and Head of Editorial.

## 3. Headlines

Our headlines suggest and invite; they never command. 

### Conventions
- **Hero Headline:** The collection name followed by a two-word descriptor. 
  - *Pattern:* [Name]: [Adjective] [Noun]
  - *Example:* Kalyani: Heritage Weave
- **Section Headlines:** Sentence case, sensory, grounded in the cloth.
  - *Example:* The geometry of the loom
- **Character Limits:** 
  - Hero Headline: Max 40 characters.
  - Section Headline: Max 60 characters.

## 4. Descriptions

The description sets the pace of the page. It transitions the woman from arrival to curiosity.

### Short Description (Grid Cards & Previews)
- **Length:** 15–25 words.
- **Style:** Sensory, specific, one breath.
- **Example:** Mulberry silk and hand-spun zari. A study in the weight of memory.

### Long Description (Detail Page)
- **Length:** 2–3 paragraphs (maximum 25 words per sentence).
- **Style:** Narrative arc (Arrival → Curiosity → Wonder). Grounded in origin and technique.
- **Example:** Woven in the quiet rhythm of Molakalmuru, this cloth carries memory. The temple borders anchor the drape, while the hand-spun zari catches the ceremonial light. 

It is made to outlive its first wearer. Every thread is a deliberate choice, requiring eighty hours on the loom.

### What Makes a Good Description
Specificity. Name the weave, the region, the touch of the silk. Do not rely on abstract praise.

## 5. Grid Copy

The product grid must remain quiet. No visual noise.

- **Product Name:** The specific name of the saree (e.g., The Midnight Jasmine Saree).
- **Hover State:** Reveal the weaver's name and region (e.g., Woven by Lakshmi in Kanchipuram).
- **Price Display:** Clean, unadorned formatting. No strike-throughs. No sale tags ever.

## 6. Filters

Filters must align with the HOP voice—refined and intentional. 

### Approved Filter Labels
- **By Craft:** Kanchipuram, Molakalmuru, Jamdani, Ikat
- **By Thread:** Mulberry Silk, Linen, Organza, Cotton
- **By Weight:** Gossamer, Mid-weight, Archival Weight
- **By Motif:** Temple, Geometric, Floral, Plain

### Unapproved Filters
- "Occasion" (Never tell her where to wear it)
- "Trending" or "Bestsellers"
- "Price: Low to High" (Sort, do not filter by cheapness)

## 7. Sorting Labels

We do not use aggressive commerce sorting. 

### Approved Sort Options
- **Default:** A Curated View
- **Chronology:** Newest Additions
- **Craft:** By Weaver Region

*Note: Tone remains warm, reserved, and precise.*

## 8. Collection Introductions

Every collection opens with an editorial introduction that sets the emotional tone.

- **Kalyani:** "Rooted in ceremony and the heavy drape of memory. These are archival weights, woven to be passed down."
- **Viara:** "Sheer silk and evening light. A quiet presence for the hours between dusk and dawn."
- **Arya:** "The daily rhythm. Linen and tight-warp silks that move with the cadence of the working day."
- **Padma:** "Fine lines and geometric intent. Where the discipline of the loom meets the poetry of the grid."
- **Spandana:** "Contemporary breath. The handloom vocabulary, evolved for the modern collector."

## 9. Editorial Rules

- **Do:** Lead with story. Use sensory language. Name the region. Introduce the weaver.
- **Do:** Write for System 1 (sensory, emotional) before System 2 (specifications, dimensions).
- **Don't:** List features like a catalog. 
- **Don't:** Use urgency ("Selling fast"). 
- **Don't:** Use generic adjectives ("Gorgeous", "Stunning"). Refer to the [Approved Words list](../04-vocabulary-system/approved-words.md).

## 10. Consistency Rules

- **Cross-Collection:** The grid structure, typography, and image pacing must remain identical across all collections to maintain the House's quiet visual rhythm.
- **Differentiation:** The color palette (using the Coastal Blossom Philosophy proportions) and emotional tone of the introductory copy are what differentiate the collections.

## 11. Collection Metadata

Every collection requires specific metadata for SEO and internal architecture.

- **Name:** The collection name (e.g., Kalyani).
- **Tagline:** A 3-4 word essence (e.g., The Heritage Weave).
- **Short Description:** 15-25 words. Sensory hook.
- **Long Description:** 50-80 words. The complete narrative arc.
- **SEO Title:** House of Padmavati | [Name] Collection
- **SEO Description:** 150-160 characters. Clear, restrained, weaving technique and origin.

## 12. The Five Collections: Editorial Specs

### Kalyani
- **Tagline:** Wedding Silk · Heritage Weave
- **Material Focus:** Heavyweight Mulberry silk, hand-spun zari.
- **Motif Focus:** Traditional temple borders.
- **Origin:** Molakalmuru and Kanchipuram.
- **Editorial Tone:** Archival, reverent, substantial. "This cloth carries memory."

### Viara
- **Tagline:** Reception Silk · Quiet Presence
- **Material Focus:** Fine silks, sheer organzas, tissue.
- **Motif Focus:** Minimalist, translucent details.
- **Origin:** Chanderi, Banaras.
- **Editorial Tone:** Ethereal, soft, breathless. "Evening light woven into cloth."

### Arya
- **Tagline:** Working Silk · Daily Rhythm
- **Material Focus:** Linen, tight-warp silks, breathable cotton-silk blends.
- **Motif Focus:** Solid colors, subtle checks, understated borders.
- **Origin:** Bhagalpur, Bengal.
- **Editorial Tone:** Grounded, practical, moving. "Made for the cadence of the day."

### Padma
- **Tagline:** The Geometric Grid
- **Material Focus:** Crisp cottons, structural silks.
- **Motif Focus:** Fine lines, checks, structural and architectural grids.
- **Origin:** Chettinad, Kota.
- **Editorial Tone:** Precise, deliberate, focused. "The mathematics of the loom."

### Spandana
- **Tagline:** Contemporary Expression
- **Material Focus:** Experimental blends, untraditional yarn combinations.
- **Motif Focus:** Abstract, negative space, broken symmetry.
- **Origin:** Various master weavers pushing boundaries.
- **Editorial Tone:** Forward-looking, dynamic, artistic. "The evolving language of thread."
