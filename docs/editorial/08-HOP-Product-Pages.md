## Overview

At House of Padmavati (HOP), the product detail page is an editorial space first, and a point of commerce second. We do not sell; we present. The page is designed to invite the woman into the story of the cloth, respecting her discernment and allowing her to choose with intention. Every element—from the product narrative to the microcopy—must serve this goal of quiet luxury and cultural custodianship.

---

## 1. Product Narrative

### Philosophy
Every saree has a story. The story comes before the specifications. We do not begin with the weight of the silk or the density of the zari; we begin with the feeling of the drape, the history of the weave, and the quiet dignity of the craft. 

### Narrative Arc
The product story follows a specific emotional trajectory:
1. **Opening (Sensory Hook):** Ground the reader in the physical reality of the cloth (the rustle, the weight, the smell of the loom).
2. **Context (Where this belongs):** Suggest a moment, a mood, or a season without prescribing an occasion. 
3. **Craft (How it was made):** Introduce the human hands and deliberate slowness behind the weave.
4. **Continuance (How it endures):** Position the saree as a living heirloom, meant to outlast its first wearer.

### System 1 vs. System 2
- **System 1 (Emotional Story):** Appears first. This is the primary narrative.
- **System 2 (Rational Specs):** Available on demand via a quiet, unobtrusive accordion interface below the story.

### How to Write a Product Story
Use the narrative arc to craft 2-3 short, evocative paragraphs. Average sentence length should be 12-18 words. Rely on specific nouns and gentle verbs. 

*Template Idea:* Begin with the physical sensation. Move into the intention behind the color or motif. Conclude with a nod to the weaver's dedication or the saree's future legacy.

## 2. Title System

Our naming convention balances poetic resonance with absolute clarity.

- **Product Name Format:** [Name] — [Descriptor]
- **Example:** Megham — Twilight Grey Pure Linen Saree

### Naming Conventions
- Names are always proper nouns (women's names, rivers, raagas, natural phenomena) drawn from Indian heritage, never descriptive adjectives.
- The descriptor clarifies the material and the fundamental nature of the product.
- **Evocation:** The title should evoke a sense of quiet grace and specific identity.
- **Character Limits:** The entire title string should rarely exceed 60 characters to maintain visual restraint.

## 3. Descriptions

### Short Description
**Purpose:** For collection cards, search results, and sharing previews.
**Length:** 15-25 words.
**Format:** A single, evocative sentence that captures the essence and material of the saree.
*Example (Megham):* "A twilight grey linen saree, woven with quiet intent for days that ask for lightness."

### Product Story (Main Editorial)
**Purpose:** The primary narrative on the product page.
**Length:** 2-3 paragraphs.
**Format:** Sensory, specific, deeply rooted in the House's principles. 
*Example (Viara Silk):* 
"There is a distinct weight to the Viara silk, one that grounds the wearer the moment it settles on the shoulder. It is not a cloth that asks for attention; rather, it commands a quiet presence in the room.

Handwoven over fourteen days in Kanchipuram, the border carries the memory of temple architecture, articulated in pure silver zari. It is an exercise in restraint—a saree designed to age beautifully, softening with every drape and every season."

## 4. Specifications

### What to Include
Specifications anchor our claims in reality. They build trust.
- Fabric composition
- Weight (e.g., 650g)
- Dimensions (e.g., 5.5m saree + 0.8m unstitched blouse piece)
- Weave type
- Origin (specific village or region)

### Presentation
Specifications are **never inline** with the emotional story. They reside in a dedicated "The Weave" accordion. 

### "The Weave" Accordion Content Structure
- **Origin:** Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu
- **Craft:** Hand-plied Mulberry Silk, Korvai weave
- **Time to Weave:** 14 days
- **Dimensions:** 5.5 meters, accompanied by an 0.8 meter blouse piece
- **Weight:** 650 grams

## 5. Care Instructions

### Philosophy
We do not speak of "maintenance"; we speak of preservation. Caring for a handwoven saree is a deliberate ritual, an act of honoring the craft and ensuring continuance.

### Presentation
Detailed instructions live in the "Saree Care" accordion.

### Care Instruction Copy Template
*Template:* "This cloth carries memory. To preserve its weave and luster, we suggest..."

### Approved Instructions by Fabric Type
- **Silk:** "Dry clean only. Store wrapped in the provided cotton muslin, away from direct sunlight. We suggest unfolding and airing the silk quietly every few months to let the natural fibers breathe."
- **Linen/Cotton:** "Gentle hand wash in cold water with a mild, natural soap. Dry in the shade. The weave will naturally soften and find its unique drape with time."

### Tone
Warm, specific, ceremonial. Never clinical.

## 6. Craft Information

### What to Include
- Specific weave technique (e.g., Korvai, Jamdani)
- Loom type (e.g., Pit loom, Frame loom)
- Weaver region or specific cluster
- Time required to weave

### Where It Appears
- Woven naturally into the **Product Story** (e.g., "Handwoven over fourteen days...").
- Formally listed in **The Weave** accordion.

### How to Present It
Specific, human-scale, and rooted in the real. We say "fourteen days on a traditional pit loom," not "meticulously handcrafted by artisans."

## 7. Shipping

### Structure
Information must be clear and readily accessible via an accordion or distinct section below the fold.

### Approved Copy
"We prepare your saree with quiet intention. Every order is carefully wrapped in jasmine-scented paper and bound with cotton ribbon, free of plastic."

### Dispatch Timeline
"Please allow 3 to 5 business days for us to prepare and dispatch your parcel. We will send a note with your tracking details once it begins its journey to you."

### Tone
Clear, transparent, warm. We do not promise "express shipping" because the experience of receiving a HOP piece cannot be rushed.

---

## 8. Returns

### Structure
Brief, clear policy statement available on the product page via an accordion.

### Approved Copy
"We wish for you to feel completely assured in your choice. Should this saree not find its place with you, we accept returns in unworn condition within 14 days of receipt."

### Tone
Reassuring, gentle, completely free of legalistic or combative language.

## 9. Trust Building

### Trust Signals
- Naming the specific region and technique
- Stating the exact time to weave
- Clear, un-retouched photography
- Transparent material composition

### What Builds Trust
- Specificity (System 2 details).
- Admitting the reality of the craft (e.g., acknowledging slight variations in hand-dyeing).

### What Undermines Trust
- Generic praise ("exquisite," "stunning").
- Hiding shipping costs or return policies.
- Urgency tactics ("Only 2 left!").

## 10. Microcopy

Every word is a choice. The microcopy must support the House's slow, intentional philosophy.

- **Primary Action:** Add to bag (Never "Add to cart")
- **Secondary Action:** Save to wishlist
- **Size/Variant Selectors:** Clear, sentence case.
- **Stock Status:** Simply state "Available" or "Archived/Sold out." **Never** show stock counts. **Never** use "Hurry!" or "Selling fast."
- **Price Display:** Clean, without styling emphasis. **No discounts ever.** Never show a "compare at" price.
- **Delivery Estimate:** "Expected arrival: [Date range]"

## 11. CTA Language

### Approved Language
- **Primary CTA:** Add to bag
- **Secondary CTA:** Save to wishlist
- **Exploration CTA:** Browse the collection

### Forbidden Language
- Add to cart
- Buy now
- Shop now
- Get it today
- Hurry
- Claim yours

## 12. Information Hierarchy

The page is structured to guide the wearer from emotion to intention, and finally to logistics.

1. **First View (Above the fold):** High-fidelity, un-retouched imagery. Product Title, Price, "Add to bag" button. The visual breathes (Jasmine Mist).
2. **Second View (Just below):** The Product Story (System 1). The emotional and sensory narrative of the piece.
3. **Third View (The Accordions):** System 2 details. 
   - *The Weave* (Specifications & Craft)
   - *Saree Care* (Preservation instructions)
   - *Shipping & Returns* (Logistics)

This balance ensures the page remains emotionally resonant without sacrificing the technical transparency required to build enduring trust.
