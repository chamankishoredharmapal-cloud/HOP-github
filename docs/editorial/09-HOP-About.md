# About Page Strategy

**Status:** Production
**Last Updated:** July 2026

## 1. Purpose
The About page answers 'Why does House of Padmavati exist?' and 'Who are the people behind the cloth?' It is not a corporate history page. It is a story of origin, values, and craft.

- **Primary Emotion:** Reverence
- **Secondary Emotion:** Belonging
- **Objective:** To build deep trust and emotional resonance by sharing the human reality behind the digital house.

## 2. Narrative
The origin story is centred on Padmavati — the real woman the house is named after. She represents the archetype of our wearer: a woman who understands that true value lies in quietness, continuance, and intention.

**Narrative Arc:** Origin → Philosophy → Craft → Values → Invitation

The story must feel like a quiet conversation. It is a personal truth, shared with someone who understands its weight. We do not boast; we invite.

## 3. Timeline
We do not present a corporate timeline or a rigid chronological list of milestones. The history of the house is woven naturally into the narrative flow. Time is measured in generations of weavers and the life span of a well-made garment, not financial quarters or launch dates.

## 4. Mission
**Core Mission:** "To preserve and evolve the handwoven saree as a living art form."

On the page, this mission is never stated as a corporate declaration. Instead, it is shown through our commitment to the loom, our deliberate pace, and our dedication to generational thinking. It is an understood undercurrent in every sentence.

## 5. Vision
**Core Vision:** "The most respected digital fashion house for Indian handloom."

Like the mission, the vision is implied. It appears through the unwavering standard of our presentation, the absence of marketing noise, and the reverence with which we treat the cloth. We do not ask for respect; we command it through quiet excellence.

## 6. Craft
The About page communicates the broader craft story — the philosophy of how and why we make. 
- We introduce The Coastal Blossom philosophy (60% Jasmine Mist, 25% Coastal Teal, 10% Golden Sand, 5% Sakura Blush) as our guiding ethos for balance, space, and depth.
- Specific, highly technical craft details (weave structures, yarn counts) belong on individual product pages. The About page holds the emotional architecture of the craft.

## 7. People
We write about the people behind the house as a collective dedicated to a shared purpose. 
- There are no corporate headshots, job titles, or hierarchical structures.
- We speak of the weavers by name when detailing specific works, but on this page, we speak of the relationship between the house, the weaver, and the wearer.
- **Pronouns:** We use "we", "our", and "the house". We never use "I", "one", or "they".

## 8. Trust
Trust is built through transparency and consistency with our brand promises. 
- We reiterate our core promises implicitly: no mass production, no urgency, deliberate choices.
- The "What We Hold To" section reframes traditional corporate values as personal, unbreakable commitments to the wearer and the craft.

## 9. Editorial Flow

**a. Page Header**
- **Content:** The main title and a framing thought.
- **Tone:** Grounded, welcoming, definitive.
- **Word Count:** 10-15 words.

**b. The Origin Story**
- **Content:** The story of Padmavati and the genesis of the house.
- **Tone:** Reverent, narrative, warm.
- **Word Count:** 80-120 words.

**c. The Coastal Blossom**
- **Content:** An introduction to our design and spatial philosophy.
- **Tone:** Reflective, sensory, balanced.
- **Word Count:** 60-90 words.

**d. What We Hold To**
- **Content:** Our values expressed as promises.
- **Tone:** Resolute, quiet, absolute.
- **Word Count:** 4-5 short commitments, 15-20 words each.

## 10. Approved Copy
*The following copy is production-ready for implementation.*

### Page Header
**Headline:** The story of the house
**Subhead:** A quiet dedication to the handwoven saree.

### The Origin Story
House of Padmavati is named for a real woman. She understood that a saree is not merely a garment, but a vessel for memory, time, and human touch. 

We built this house to honour that understanding. In a world of fleeting trends and mass production, we choose the deliberate pace of the handloom. We work with families who have passed their knowledge through generations, ensuring that every thread carries the weight of true craft. This is a space for slowness, for quietness, and for cloth made to outlive its first wearer.

### The Coastal Blossom
Our work is guided by the balance of The Coastal Blossom. We believe in the necessity of space — the breath between the threads, the quiet around the cloth. We seek depth and intention in every dye, the warmth of tradition in every motif, and the gentle softness of human imperfection. It is an aesthetic of restraint. We remove everything that does not serve the cloth or the woman who wears it.

### What We Hold To
**Time, not speed**
We refuse the rush of modern production. A handwoven saree takes weeks to emerge, and we honour that pace.

**The named weaver**
Every cloth carries the signature of its maker. We know their names, their families, and their hands.

**Quiet continuance**
We design for generations, not seasons. Our garments are meant to be worn, kept, and eventually passed on.

**Intentional presence**
No noise. No urgency. We invite you to choose deliberately and thoughtfully, without the pressure of time.
