## 1. Support Philosophy

Customer care at House of Padmavati is an extension of the house, not a service desk. Every interaction should feel like a conversation with someone who cares deeply about the cloth and the woman wearing it. 

The primary emotion we seek to evoke is safety. When a woman writes to us, she must feel heard, respected, and assured that we will handle her concerns with deliberate care.

Our response promise is simple. A human response within two business days. There are no chatbots, no automated responses, and no ticket numbers in our customer-facing communication. We write to her directly, warmly, and precisely.

## 2. Shipping

Our shipping communication must reflect the intention behind the weave. The dispatch timeline is communicated with specific, honest transparency. We do not use urgency language or make impossible promises.

### Approved Shipping Copy
"Your saree is being prepared for its journey to you. We take two business days to carefully fold and package each piece."

### Packaging Description
"Your saree arrives wrapped in jasmine-scented paper, tied with a soft cotton ribbon. A handwritten note and a keepsake card detailing its origin accompany the cloth. We use no plastic in our packaging."

### Tracking Communication Tone
Tracking updates should feel like gentle notifications from a friend, rather than automated system alerts. 

### Approved Shipping FAQ Entries
**How long does delivery take.**  
"We dispatch orders within two business days. Domestic deliveries take between three to five business days. International deliveries require seven to ten business days."

**How is the saree packaged.**  
"Every piece is folded into jasmine-scented paper and secured with a cotton ribbon. We include a personal note and ensure the packaging remains entirely free of plastic."

## 3. Returns

Our return policy must be written in plain language. We avoid legalese and defensive tones. Returning a piece should be as considered and quiet a process as receiving one.

### Return Policy Editorial Spec
We accept returns within seven days of receipt, provided the saree remains unworn and the original folds are intact. The process should feel straightforward and supported.

### Approved Return Policy Copy
"If the cloth is not right for you, we welcome returns within seven days of receipt. The saree must remain unworn, in its original condition, with all tags attached."

### Communicating Returns with Warmth
When processing a return, we express gratitude that she tried the piece. We never question her decision. 

### Return Process Steps Copy
1. "Write to us at care@houseofpadmavati.com to initiate the return."
2. "We will arrange a courier to collect the package from your address."
3. "Once the cloth returns to the house, we will process your refund within three business days."

### Approved FAQ Entries for Returns
**How do I return a saree.**  
"Please write to us within seven days of receiving your order. We will arrange a collection and guide you through the process."

## 4. Care

Saree care is about preservation, not maintenance. It is a ritual, not a chore. We guide her on how to let the cloth outlive its first wearer.

### Saree Care Section Editorial Spec
Care instructions should feel like quiet wisdom passed down across generations. 

### Care Instructions by Fabric Type

**Pure Mulberry Silk**  
"Air the cloth in a shaded room after every drape. Dry clean only when entirely necessary. Fold the saree along its natural creases to protect the zari."

**Pure Linen**  
"Hand wash in cold water with a mild, neutral soap. Dry in the shade to preserve the dye. Iron while slightly damp for the crispest finish."

**Organza**  
"This delicate weave requires a gentle hand. Dry clean only. Avoid heavy pressing, and store rolled rather than folded to prevent permanent creasing."

**Silk Blends**  
"A soft hand wash in cold water is sufficient. Never wring the cloth. Lay it flat to dry in a shaded area."

### Storage Guidance Copy
"Store your sarees in breathable cotton bags. Include dry neem leaves or cloves to protect the weave. Refold the cloth every six months to prevent the zari from settling into permanent creases."

### Seasonal Care Guidance
"During the monsoon, ensure the wardrobe remains dry. Air out the heavier silks on a clear day to allow the threads to breathe."

## 6. Policies

Policies must be written with absolute honesty. They should be plain, readable, and free from corporate jargon.

### Shipping Policy Approved Copy
"We prepare your order with deliberate care. Dispatch occurs within two business days. You will receive a tracking link once the courier collects the package. We rely on trusted partners to ensure safe transit."

### Returns Policy Approved Copy
"Returns are accepted within seven days of delivery. The piece must remain in its original, unworn condition. Once we receive and verify the cloth, we will issue a full refund to your original payment method."

### Privacy Policy Editorial Guidelines
We protect her data as carefully as we protect our craft. Explain what data is collected and why, using simple, declarative sentences. Avoid legal jargon wherever possible.

### Terms of Service Editorial Guidelines
Set clear expectations about the transaction. Maintain a tone that is respectful and transparent.

## 8. Messages

Our transactional emails are an extension of the brand voice. They are warm, precise, and reassuring.

### Response Message Templates

**Order Confirmation**  
"We have received your order. Your saree is now being prepared for you. We will write again once it is ready for dispatch."

**Dispatch Notification**  
"Your saree has begun its journey to you. You may follow its progress through the tracking link below. We hope it brings you quiet joy."

**Delivery Confirmation**  
"Your package has been delivered. We hope the cloth meets your expectations. Please refer to the enclosed card for care instructions."

**Return Acknowledgement**  
"We have received your return request. We will arrange a collection shortly. Thank you for handling the cloth with care."

**General Inquiry Response**  
"Thank you for writing to the house. We appreciate your question regarding our weaves."

### Email Tone Guidelines
Emails must be concise and warm. Use standard sentence casing and avoid unnecessary formatting.

### Opening and Closing Conventions
- **Opening:** "Dear [Name],"
- **Closing:** "Warmly, The House of Padmavati"

## 9. Reassurance Language

There will be moments of uncertainty. Our language must steady the situation.

### Language Patterns
- "We understand your concern."
- "We are looking into this for you."
- "Please allow us to correct this."

### Approved Phrases for Reassurance
- "Your order is safe with us."
- "We will ensure this reaches you."
- "We are here to assist you."

### Forbidden Phrases
- "It is out of our hands."
- "You must have made a mistake."
- "That is not our policy."

### Edge Case Handling
- **Damaged Items:** "We are deeply sorry that the piece arrived in this condition. We will arrange a replacement immediately."
- **Wrong Delivery:** "We have made an error with your order. We will collect the incorrect item and send the right piece at once."
- **Complaints:** "We hear your concern and take it seriously. We are reviewing the matter and will resolve it."
