# Editorial Brief: About Page

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose (one sentence):** Tell the origin story of House of Padmavati through the women, the place, and the craft — earning trust and emotional connection through specificity and restraint.
- **Business Goal:** Convert first-time visitors into brand-aware prospects with enough trust to browse collections.
- **Brand Goal:** Establish House of Padmavati as a cultural custodian — a house that preserves and continues a weaving tradition — not a commercial brand that sells sarees.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Reverence (page emotion map: About page primary = Reverence)
- **Secondary Emotion:** Belonging (page emotion map: About page secondary = Belonging)
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Trust → Belonging
- **Emotional Arc:** Curiosity (who is this house?) → Wonder (at the craft, the women, the place) → Trust (earned through specificity) → Belonging (she shares these values)

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well — urban professional, 28–55, values craft over trends, curated wardrobe, reads long-form, insulted by discounts
- **Reader Intent:** "Who are these people and why should I trust them?"
- **Trust Requirement:** Earn trust through named places, named weavers, specific craft details, and transparent philosophy — not through claims or badges

## Content Priorities
1. The origin — Padmavati, the real woman; the dedication that became a house
2. The place and the craft — Molakalmuru, the pit loom, the 21-day weave, the weavers who remain
3. The philosophy — what House of Padmavati believes, how it operates, what it promises

## Call to Action
- **Primary CTA:** Browse the collections (gentle invitation to explore, not a directive)
- **Secondary CTA:** None

## SEO Mandate
- **Primary Keyword:** House of Padmavati
- **SEO Intent:** Informational (brand discovery)
- **Title Constraint:** "About House of Padmavati — Handwoven Sarees" (43 chars)
- **Description Constraint:** "The origin story of House of Padmavati. A digital fashion house dedicated to handwoven Indian sarees, named after a real woman." (126 chars)

## Success Criteria
- Reader finishes the page with a clear understanding of who HOP is, where the sarees come from, and why they cost what they cost
- Reader feels she has discovered something authentic — not been sold to
- Reader navigates to collections or a craft story after reading

## Constraints
- **Brand constraints:** No self-praise ("we are a luxury brand"), no urgency, no marketing noise, no forbidden words, no exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions
- **Vocabulary focus:** Quiet, considered, hand, loom, weave, thread, generation, continues, carried, slow, deliberate, intention
- **Tone register:** Sensory (scenes 1-2), Reflective (scene 3), Trust (scene 4), Resolution (scene 5)
