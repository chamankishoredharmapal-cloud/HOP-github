# Narrative Plan: About Page

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Portrait — the brand revealed through accumulated detail
- **Tension Curve:** Gentle rise through discovery (scenes 1–3), plateau of understanding (scene 4), quiet resolution (scene 5). No sharp tension. The arc is accumulative — each scene adds a layer of the portrait until the whole picture is visible.

## Scene Sequence
| Scene | Purpose | Emotion | Sensory Anchor | Tone Register | Knowledge Function | Transition |
|-------|---------|---------|----------------|---------------|-------------------|------------|
| 1 — The Woman | Introduce Padmavati as origin. Anchor the house in a real person, not a brand concept | Wonder, reverence | The weight of silk in a woman's hands | Sensory | Reader learns: the house is named after a real woman; a dedication, not a brand invention | From a woman's hands → to the place where cloth is made |
| 2 — The Place | Ground the house in Molakalmuru. Show the loom, the light, the weavers | Wonder, awe | Morning light in a weaving shed; the rhythm of the shuttle | Sensory | Reader learns: where the sarees are woven; who weaves them; how few remain; the 21-day process | From the specific place → to the philosophy it produced |
| 3 — The Philosophy | Reveal what House of Padmavati believes. Not a mission statement — a set of choices | Reflective trust | Silence — the negative space of a page, the absence of noise | Reflective | Reader learns: quiet luxury, no discounts, no urgency, named weavers, generational thinking | From beliefs → to the concrete promises |
| 4 — The Promise | State what House of Padmavati guarantees. Specificity builds trust | Trust, confidence | The hand-folded jasmine paper of packaging; the handwritten keepsake card | Trust | Reader learns: 7 promises; transparency; what to expect | From promises → to an open door |
| 5 — The Invitation | Close with warmth. Invite her into the world without pressure | Belonging, calm | A door left open; cloth waiting to be chosen | Resolution | Reader feels: welcome, unhurried, ready to explore | Natural transition to Collections |

## Identity Transformation
- **From Identity:** A woman looking at another saree brand online
- **To Identity:** A woman who has found a house that shares her values — a place where craft is respected, weavers are named, and the cloth carries memory

## Resolution Design
- **Central Question Answered:** "Who are these people and why should I trust them?" → They are custodians of a weaving tradition, named after a real woman, making sarees the way they were once made, with no shortcuts and no sales.
- **Emotional Payoff:** The quiet satisfaction of discovering something authentic — not being sold to, but being welcomed.
- **Memory Anchor:** Gangamma at her loom at 6:30 AM. Twenty-one days. Fewer than 200 families. The saree will wait.
- **Transition to Next Page:** Gentle invitation to browse collections — "You might begin with the Kalyani collection."

## Pacing Map
- Scene 1: Slow — immersive, sensory opening
- Scene 2: Medium — detailed but alive, with sensory rhythm
- Scene 3: Slow — reflective, spacious, philosophical
- Scene 4: Medium — clear, specific, trust-building
- Scene 5: Slow — brief, warm, unhurried closure
