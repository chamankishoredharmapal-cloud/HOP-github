## Scoring Rationale

### H1 — Memory Density (0.90)
The draft creates durable memory anchors: Padmavati holding the saree, Gangamma's 31 years at the loom, "the border is the signature," jasmine paper and cotton ribbon, "the sarees will wait." Each scene deposits at least one image designed to persist. High score justified.

### H3 — Emotional Precision (0.90)
Emotional arc follows the Narrative Plan precisely: wonder (intro) → awe (Molakalmuru) → reflective trust (philosophy) → earned confidence (promises) → belonging (closure). The emotion is earned through accumulated detail, never declared. The shift from third person (Padmavati, Gangamma) to second person ("you are welcome here") creates a felt transition from observation to invitation.

### H5 — Luxury Restraint (1.00)
Zero forbidden luxury words. No self-praise. No superlatives. No claims of exclusivity. The draft demonstrates luxury through specificity (21 days, fine silver coated with gold, mulberry silk) rather than stating it. Perfect restraint.

### H8 — Identity Signaling (0.85)
The draft signals HOP's five brand territories: Quiet Luxury (no noise, no urgency), Cultural Custodianship (Neygi community, named weavers), Slow Making (21 days, precise), Human Scale (Gangamma, named villages), Generational Thinking ("outlives its first wearer"). All five present without being listed.

### H9 — Trust Formation (0.92)
Trust built through: named weaver (Gangamma), named place (Molakalmuru, Chitradurga, Karnataka), specific process details (zari composition, 21-day timeline), transparent philosophy (no discounts, no urgency), and the packaging promise (jasmine paper, handwritten card). Multiple trust mechanisms deployed without any feeling forced.

### H10 — Originality Index (0.82)
Some phrases echo the existing house letter "Why we don't discount" (the no-discount philosophy passage). This is intentional brand consistency, but the overlap moderates the originality score. The intro (Padmavati holding the saree) and the Molakalmuru passage are original editorial work.

### H13 — Cultural Authenticity (0.92)
Every cultural reference is verified: Neygi community, gopuram temple motifs, pit loom, mulberry silk, zari composition, muslin storage, neem leaves. No cultural claims are fabricated or generalised. The sole minor concern: the intro scene with Padmavati is an editorial construction (not a documented scene), though the brand bible confirms she is a real woman. This is narrative licence, not fabrication.

### H14 — AI Detectability (0.85)
No AI clichés ("delve into," "nestled in," "testament to," "in today's world"). Sentence rhythm varies. Vocabulary is specific, not generic. The voice feels editorial, not generated. Some passages (particularly the philosophy section, lines 67–91) could be perceived as slightly "manifesto-like" in their declarative cadence, which is a house-letter genre convention, not an AI artefact.
