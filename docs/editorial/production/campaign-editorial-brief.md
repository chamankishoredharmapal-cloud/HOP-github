# Editorial Brief: Campaign (The Quiet Wedding)

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Position House of Padmavati as the antithesis of stressful, heavily embellished bridal wear. Promote heirloom handlooms for weddings.
- **Business Goal:** Drive high-AOV bridal purchases and custom bridal styling appointments.
- **Brand Goal:** Define "Quiet Luxury" in the context of Indian weddings.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Desire.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Anticipation (Relief).
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Desire.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Bride or Mother of the Bride (The Woman Who Chooses Well).
- **Reader Intent:** Looking for something deeply traditional but comfortable and understated for a wedding.
- **Trust Requirement:** Must not sound like a discount "Bridal Sale." Must sound like a curatorial edit.

## Content Priorities
1. **The Core Tension:** Weight vs. Lightness. Show how a handloom saree has the visual weight of heritage but the physical lightness of breathable fabric.
2. **Re-wearability:** Emphasize that these are not garments worn once and boxed away forever. They are meant to be worn to other weddings and eventually passed down.
3. **Pacing:** Let the imagery breathe. Use short, poetic copy blocks.
