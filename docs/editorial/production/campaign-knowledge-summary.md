## Campaign Concept
**Theme:** "The Quiet Wedding"
**Concept:** Moving away from loud, heavily embellished, heavy bridal wear. Focusing on heirloom handloom sarees (Kanchipuram, Banarasi, Linen) that allow the bride to actually *move*, *breathe*, and *be present* on her day, and wear the garment again in the future.

## Deliverables Required
1. **Campaign Page Content:** React page at `src/pages/campaigns/QuietWedding.tsx`.
2. **Email Copy:** An announcement email template for the campaign.
3. **Social Copy:** Three Instagram caption drafts for the campaign launch.

## Knowledge Gaps
- None. We will draw on existing collections (Kalyani, Viara) to curate the campaign.

## Editorial Constraints
- **Primary Emotion:** Desire.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Anticipation.
- **Arc:** Transformation (Loud/Stressful → Quiet/Present).
- **No forbidden words:** Luxury, Premium, Exquisite, Perfect, Magic, Authentic.
