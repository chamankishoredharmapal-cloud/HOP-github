# Narrative Plan: Campaign (The Quiet Wedding)

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Transformation Arc.
- **Tension Curve:** Starts with the tension of modern wedding expectations (heavy, loud, restrictive) and resolves into the relief of a quiet, breathable handloom heritage.

## Page Sequence (`src/pages/campaigns/QuietWedding.tsx`)
1. **Hero:** Expansive imagery. Title: "The Quiet Wedding". Subtitle: "Presence over performance."
2. **The Thesis (Copy Block):** Challenge the idea that a bride must be weighed down to be beautiful. Introduce the handloom alternative.
3. **The Edit (Curated Products):** Showcase 3-4 specific sarees (e.g., a heavy zari Kanchipuram from Kalyani, a tissue silk from Viara) framed as bridal alternatives.
4. **The Second Life:** A section highlighting that these sarees are not meant for a single day. They are meant to be worn, folded, rested, and worn again to other celebrations.
5. **Call to Action:** "Book a bridal consultation" or "Explore the Edit".

## Email Copy Sequence
- **Subject:** The Quiet Wedding Edit
- **Body:** An invitation to reconsider bridal wear. "A wedding is a moment of profound transition. Your garment should not distract you from it." Link to the campaign page.

## Social Copy Sequence
1. **Post 1 (Teaser):** Focus on the weight of a garment vs the lightness of the weave.
2. **Post 2 (The Edit):** Showcase a specific saree from the edit. Discuss the zari work.
3. **Post 3 (Philosophy):** A quote about heirloom re-wearability.

## Core Copy Directives
- **Remove:** "Bridal Collection", "Wedding Season Sale", "Must-have".
- **Formatting:** Clean React page with full-bleed imagery and large typography.
