# Collection Landing Page — Editorial Copy

## Delivery Format
This document contains the editorial copy for the `/collections` landing page. It is structured for three consumption points:
1. **Page intro** → hardcoded in `src/pages/Collections.tsx` (heading, subheading, intro paragraph)
2. **Per-collection cards** → stored in Supabase `collections.editorial_story` and `collections.tagline` fields
3. **Closing section** → hardcoded in `src/pages/Collections.tsx` (pull-quote + CTA)

---

## 1. Page Intro

### Subheading (tracking label)
The Atelier

### Heading (h1)
Five collections, one quiet house.

### Intro Paragraph
Every Padmavati saree belongs to one of five families. Each is named after a woman — not a category, not a season, not a trend. The names arrive without explanation. The sarees reveal their character in the wearing.

## 2. Collection Cards

### 2.1 Kalyani

**Tagline:** Wedding Silk · Heritage Weave

**Editorial Story:**
Kalyani is named for the feeling of a winter wedding — the one where the whole family gathers after years, where the light fades by five, where the evening stretches long and warm. Temple border sarees woven in Molakalmuru on a pit loom, in pure Mulberry silk with hand-spun zari. Twenty-one days per drape. Each one carries a weight of occasion — not heavy, but felt.

---

### 2.2 Viara

**Tagline:** Reception Silk · Quiet Presence

**Editorial Story:**
Viara announces itself without raising its voice. These sarees are woven for evenings that demand a certain kind of attention — not loud, but undeniable. A winter reception in Udaipur. A gallery opening in the monsoon. A milestone birthday in Chennai. The silk that outlasts the conversation and is remembered the next morning.

---

### 2.3 Arya

**Tagline:** Working Silk · Daily Rhythm

**Editorial Story:**
Arya is a rhythm, not a category. Light drapes that hold their shape through a long day. Linens that breathe from boardroom to evening. Cottons that feel like a second skin. Arya belongs to the woman who moves through her day in layers — and needs her cloth to keep pace without complaint.

---

### 2.4 Padma

**Tagline:** Geometric Weave · Structure

**Editorial Story:**
Padma is our weave laboratory — a collection born at the intersection of craft and mathematics. Every saree begins with a geometric question: what happens when the thread count shifts by one, when the pattern repeats at a different interval. The result is architectural, composed, and deliberate. Each drape is a small thesis on the beauty of structure.

---

### 2.5 Spandana

**Tagline:** Contemporary Weave · Self-Expression

**Editorial Story:**
Spandana is where the saree steps out of tradition and into the contemporary frame. Unconventional drapes. Experimental textures. Colours that refuse to sit quietly. These sarees are worn because she chooses to — not because the calendar told her to. Confidence, woven.
