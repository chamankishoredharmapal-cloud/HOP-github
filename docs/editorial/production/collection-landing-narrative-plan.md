# Narrative Plan: Collection Landing Page

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Journey — the reader moves through five curated worlds
- **Tension Curve:** Low, even, invitational. No dramatic peaks. Each collection card is a doorway, not a destination. The tension is in the reader's growing desire to choose one and enter.

## Scene Sequence
| Scene | Purpose | Emotion | Sensory Anchor | Tone Register | Transition |
|-------|---------|---------|----------------|---------------|------------|
| 1 — Intro | Frame the five collections as a curated universe. Establish that names are women, not labels | Curiosity, fascination | The quietness of a room with five doors | Reflective | From the frame → into the first collection |
| 2 — Kalyani card | Present the wedding collection. Warmth, ceremony, heritage | Wonder, reverence | Late afternoon gold before a winter ceremony | Sensory | → next collection |
| 3 — Viara card | Present the flagship/reception collection. Quiet confidence, undeniable presence | Desire, confidence | A winter reception hall, the memory of what she wore | Sensory | → next collection |
| 4 — Arya card | Present the modern woman collection. Rhythm, movement, ease | Recognition, belonging | Morning boardroom light to evening sea air | Sensory | → next collection |
| 5 — Padma card | Present the geometric/structural collection. Precision, intellect | Wonder, curiosity | The intersection of thread and mathematics | Sensory | → next collection |
| 6 — Spandana card | Present the contemporary collection. Boldness, self-expression | Desire, confidence | The choice to wear the saree on her own terms | Sensory | → closing |
| 7 — Close | Quiet resolution with pull-quote and link to the brand story | Belonging, trust | A saree is chosen, then carried | Resolution | → About page or browsing |

## Identity Transformation
- **From Identity:** A woman looking at a list of saree categories
- **To Identity:** A woman who has discovered five distinct characters and is drawn to one — the beginning of a relationship with a collection that matches her own voice

## Resolution Design
- **Central Question Answered:** "What collections does HOP offer?" → Five collections, named after women, each with a different rhythm. She chooses not from a catalogue, but from a cast of characters.
- **Emotional Payoff:** The feeling of curation — that this house has been thoughtfully assembled, not stocked.
- **Memory Anchor:** "Five collections, one quiet house." Each collection is a woman, not a category.
- **Transition to Next Page:** She clicks "Explore Kalyani" (or whichever collection resonates) and enters the collection detail page.

## Pacing Map
- Scene 1 (Intro): Slow, spacious — two short paragraphs maximum
- Scenes 2–6 (Collection cards): Medium, even rhythm — each card is tagline + 2–3 sentences
- Scene 7 (Close): Slow, brief — pull-quote + one-line CTA
