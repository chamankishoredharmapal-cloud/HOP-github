## Scoring Rationale

### Truth & Accuracy (0.95)
- All Kalyani facts verified against content unit (Molakalmuru, pit loom, Mulberry silk, hand-spun zari, 21 days)
- Other collection descriptions sourced from brand-bible.md and Supabase seed data
- No fabricated claims. Slight deduction: Viara, Arya, Padma, Spandana lack detailed weaver attribution (documented as knowledge gap, not an error)

### Voice Fidelity (0.92)
- All 5 voice dimensions hit targets (Formality 3, Warmth 3, Sensory 4, Technical 2, Urgency 0)
- Sentence rhythm follows short-medium-long-short pattern
- Slight deduction: Some collection descriptions (Arya, Padma) lean slightly more declarative than sensory

### Emotional Precision (0.90)
- Primary (Wonder) and secondary (Desire) emotions clearly triggered
- Each collection activates a distinct emotional response
- Curiosity → Wonder transition accomplished through the naming convention reveal

### Narrative Craft (0.88)
- Five-card structure creates even pacing
- Each card has a distinct character voice
- Slight deduction: Collection cards don't have the depth of multi-scene narrative arcs (appropriate for card format)

### Vocabulary Compliance (1.00)
- Zero forbidden words. Three original taglines rewritten to remove forbidden terms ("Luxury", "Fashion Forward")

### Luxury Restraint (1.00)
- Zero self-praise. Zero superlatives. Signal quality through specificity only.

### Cultural Authenticity (0.92)
- Collections named after Indian women's names (culturally grounded)
- Craft and geography references are accurate and respectful
- Occasions and settings are culturally specific (winter wedding, Udaipur reception, Chennai birthday)
