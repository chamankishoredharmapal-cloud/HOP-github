# Editorial Brief: Collection Detail (remaining)

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Tell the stories of the Arya and Viara collections, framing their specific use-cases (Daily working structure vs Evening presence) without using hierarchical labels like "casual" or "party wear."
- **Business Goal:** Drive product discovery by contextualising the collections for the user's specific lifestyle needs.
- **Brand Goal:** Position each collection as a deliberate curation rather than arbitrary seasonal drops.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Wonder. (The realisation that a saree can be tailored to a specific phase of a woman's day).
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust.
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Curiosity → Wonder.
- **Emotional Arc:** The reader sees the collection (Hero) → sees herself in the collection's intent (The Woman) → understands why the fabric behaves as it does (The Craft).

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** "Why should I choose a saree from this collection?"
- **Trust Requirement:** Avoid fashion-industry cliches ("day to night," "effortlessly chic"). Ground the collections in physical reality—friction, light, density, drape.

## Content Priorities
1. **The Woman Block:** Define the psychological intent of the collection. For Arya: Structure, competence, quiet focus. For Viara: Holding space, evening light, deliberate presence.
2. **The Craft Block:** Explain the weaving choices that support the psychological intent (e.g., Arya's resilient warp, Viara's fluid drape).
3. **Restraint:** No sales urgency.

1. **Arya Collection:** Fleshing out `src/content/collections/arya/index.md` from its stub.
2. **Viara Collection:** Fleshing out `src/content/collections/viara/index.md` from its stub.
