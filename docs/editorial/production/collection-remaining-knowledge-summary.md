## Collection Roster

### 1. Arya Collection
- **Tagline:** Working Silk · Daily Rhythm
- **Purpose:** Silks designed for daily structure. Less ornate borders, focus on breathability, movement, and enduring the friction of a workday.
- **Narrative:** The woman this collection is for values quiet competence. She doesn't dress for an audience; she dresses for her own structure. The craft focuses on tight, resilient weaves (perhaps dobby or plain weave with minimal zari).

### 2. Viara Collection
- **Tagline:** Reception Silk · Quiet Presence
- **Purpose:** Silks designed for formal presence. Richer than Arya, but less heavy than bridal (Kalyani). Designed for evenings, receptions, and significant but not center-stage moments.
- **Narrative:** The woman this collection is for holds space without needing to shout. She values drape and the way light interacts with the silk in evening environments. Craft focuses on fine warp counts and deeper, more lustrous dyes.
