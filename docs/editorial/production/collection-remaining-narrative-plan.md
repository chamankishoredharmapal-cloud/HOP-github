# Narrative Plan: Collection Detail (remaining)

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Journey Arc.
- **Tension Curve:** Observation (Hero) → Identification (The Woman) → Technical Proof (The Craft) → Resolution (The Invitation).

## Scene Sequence
| Scene | Element | Purpose | Emotion | Tone Register |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Hero | Establish the visual and conceptual anchor. | Wonder | Sensory |
| 2 | The Woman | Define the mindset of the wearer. | Connection | Reflective |
| 3 | The Craft | Explain how the physical fabric supports that mindset. | Trust | Technical/Sensory |
| 4 | The Invitation | Provide a calm closure without aggressive CTA. | Resolution | Reflective |

## Individual Story Focus

### 1. Arya Collection
- **Hero:** Working Silk · Daily Rhythm
- **The Woman:** Focuses on the woman whose day requires movement, endurance, and quiet competence. The saree is her structure.
- **The Craft:** Explains the resilient weave, the tight warp tension, and the lack of heavy zari to ensure the fabric breathes during long hours.
- **The Invitation:** A note on finding rhythm in the everyday.

### 2. Viara Collection
- **Hero:** Reception Silk · Quiet Presence
- **The Woman:** Focuses on the woman who holds a room. The saree is designed for evening light, for conversations that matter.
- **The Craft:** Explains the fluid drape, the deeper dyes designed to catch artificial light, and the subtle, deliberate placement of zari.
- **The Invitation:** A note on the weight of an evening.
