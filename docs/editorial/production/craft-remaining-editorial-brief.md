# Editorial Brief: Craft Story (remaining)

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Educate the reader on handloom linen and organza hand-embroidery.
- **Business Goal:** Build trust in HOP's material choices and justify the price point/dispatch wait times for non-silk, craft-heavy products.
- **Brand Goal:** Position HOP as a deeply knowledgeable custodian of craft, not just a retailer of sarees.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Wonder (at the physical mechanics of the craft).
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust (in HOP's expertise).
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Wonder → Trust.
- **Emotional Arc:** The reader moves from seeing the finished product to understanding the intense, deliberate physical labour required to create it.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** "What is the difference between this linen and regular linen? Why does embroidery take 14 days?"
- **Trust Requirement:** Use specific technical terms (warp, weft, elasticity, topography) but explain them clearly. Do not use generic praise like "intricate" or "beautiful."

## Content Priorities
1. **Instruction Arc:** Each note must clearly teach the reader something specific about how the fabric is made.
2. **Sensory Density (4/5):** Describe the friction of linen yarn on a loom, the sharp topography of silk thread on organza.
3. **Cross-linking:** The handloom linen note must link to the Megham product; the embroidery note must link to the Sakura product.
