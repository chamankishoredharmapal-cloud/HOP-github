## Craft Topics to Cover

### 1. Handloom Linen (`handloom-linen`)
- **What it is:** Pure linen woven on a handloom, distinct from rigid machine-milled linen.
- **Process:** The linen yarn is less elastic than silk. Weaving it by hand requires a slower, more deliberate rhythm to prevent breakage. 
- **Why it matters:** Hand-woven linen breathes better and softens immediately upon draping, whereas machine linen holds rigid angles. It's meant for the friction of a long day.

### 2. Topography of Thread: Hand-Embroidery (`hand-embroidery`)
- **What it is:** Hand-embroidery on sheer organza.
- **Process:** Passing a needle and heavy silk thread through sheer, delicate organza without pulling the warp or weft out of alignment. Requires immense tension control and up to 14 days for a single pallu.
- **Why it matters:** Creates a tactile contrast (the topography of the thread against the sheer airiness of the organza). Framing time as a feature, not a delay.
