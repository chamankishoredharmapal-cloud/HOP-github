## Craft Topics

### 1. Understanding Zari
- **Status:** Draft exists in repo (`src/content/craft-notes/understanding-zari`).
- **Core Truths:** Real zari is silver wire spun around silk, washed in gold. Imitation is copper with lacquer. Real softens with age, imitation stiffens and flakes.
- **Verification:** Accurate based on craft research. 

### 2. The Pit Loom
- **Status:** To be drafted (`src/content/craft-notes/the-pit-loom`).
- **Core Truths:** The loom is built into a pit dug into the ground to house treadles, providing leverage and a low center of gravity. It produces fabric with a human rhythm, meaning slight irregularities that machines cannot replicate. Used extensively in Molakalmuru and Ilkal.
- **Verification:** Sourced directly from `weaving-techniques.md` executive summary and technical breakdown.
