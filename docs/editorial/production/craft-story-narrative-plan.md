# Narrative Plan: Craft Story (First 2)

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Instruction Arc — knowledge transferred systematically.
- **Tension Curve:** Misconception/Curiosity (Hook) → Mechanical Reality (Process) → Outcome (Why it matters).

## Individual Story Focus

### 1. Understanding Zari
- **Hook:** The difference between a saree that glows and one that merely shines.
- **Mechanics:** Silver wire drawn through holes, wrapped around silk, washed in gold.
- **Human Element:** Breaking a wire means starting over.
- **Outcome:** Real zari softens with age; imitation stiffens and flakes.

### 2. The Pit Loom
- **Hook:** A saree is not woven by a machine; it is woven by a body in motion.
- **Mechanics:** The loom is built into the earth. The weaver sits on a plank, feet in the pit operating treadles.
- **Human Element:** The rhythm of the feet and hands dictates the tension of the cloth.
- **Outcome:** The slight irregularities are the signature of a human being, making the fabric breathe and drape better than machine-milled cloth.
