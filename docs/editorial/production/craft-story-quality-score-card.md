## Scoring Rationale

### Truth & Accuracy (1.00)
- Deeply accurate technical descriptions of both zari manufacturing and pit loom mechanics, directly sourced from executive research documents.

### Voice Fidelity (0.95)
- Strong instructional arc that maintains warmth and avoids dry academic tone.

### Emotional Precision (0.95)
- Moves gracefully from Wonder ("A saree is not woven by a machine") to Trust ("The earth absorbs the shock...").

### Narrative Craft (0.95)
- The use of the "human rhythm" as the concluding payoff for the pit loom story perfectly anchors the craft in human effort.

### Vocabulary Compliance (1.00)
- Zero forbidden words across both craft stories.

### Luxury Restraint (1.00)
- Value is justified purely through the time, skill, and physical mechanics of the weave. No artificial prestige markers used.

### Cultural Authenticity (1.00)
- Honors the lived reality of weavers in Molakalmuru and Ilkal without exploiting their labor for sentimental marketing.
