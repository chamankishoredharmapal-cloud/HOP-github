# Narrative Plan: Email Templates

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Short-form Revelation.
- **Tension Curve:** Peaks at "Shipped", resolves at "Delivered".

## Email Sequences

### 1. Welcome
- **Subject:** A quiet welcome to the house.
- **Body:** Acknowledge their arrival. Do not offer a discount code immediately. Introduce the philosophy of slow weaving and lasting garments.

### 2. Order Confirmed
- **Subject:** Your piece has been claimed.
- **Body:** Validate their choice. Move away from "Order #1234 Received." Use language that implies the garment is now resting before its journey.

### 3. Payment Success/Failed
- **Subject:** Payment secured / A pause in your order.
- **Body:** Keep it strictly operational but polite. 

### 4. Shipped
- **Subject:** From the atelier to your hands.
- **Body:** Describe the box leaving the physical space of Pondicherry. Reference the muslin and jasmine.

### 5. Delivered
- **Subject:** Your saree has arrived.
- **Body:** A gentle nudge to open the box in natural light and feel the weave before trying it on. 

### 6. Nurture (Journal / Editorial)
- **Subject:** Notes from the loom.
- **Body:** Lead with a craft story or a weaver portrait. One quiet call to action to read more.

### 7. Password Reset
- **Subject:** A key to your account.
- **Body:** Highly operational, single clear link.
