## Scoring Rationale

### Truth & Accuracy (1.00)
- All required payload interfaces and data bindings map accurately to the HTML strings.

### Voice Fidelity (1.00)
- Dramatically shifts the tone of transactional emails from automated and loud to human and quiet.

### Emotional Precision (1.00)
- The delivery email ("open it in natural light, unwrap the muslin slowly") turns a mundane transaction into an emotional ritual.

### Narrative Craft (0.95)
- Subject lines are particularly strong: "A quiet welcome to the house," "Your piece has been claimed."

### Vocabulary Compliance (1.00)
- Zero forbidden words used.

### Luxury Restraint (1.00)
- Restraint is the core feature of these emails. They refuse to yell, refusing to offer immediate discount codes or aggressively push upsells.
