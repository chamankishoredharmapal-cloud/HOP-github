# Editorial Brief: FAQ

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Answer common logistical and care questions in a way that reduces friction and builds trust.
- **Business Goal:** Decrease support tickets regarding shipping, returns, and care while increasing conversion confidence.
- **Brand Goal:** Prove that HOP handles logistical matters with the same care and precision as they weave their textiles.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Confidence. The reader must feel secure in their purchase.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust. Transparency in policies builds trust.
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Evaluation → Purchase.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** Looking for reassurance before making a high-value purchase. "If I buy this, what happens next?"

## Content Priorities
1. **Clarity over cleverness:** The answers must be definitive. Do not bury the policy in prose.
2. **Tone:** Warm, authoritative, transparent.
3. **Depth:** Go beyond generic e-commerce answers. Explain *why* something is done (e.g., *why* we don't iron the crease).
