## FAQ Topics to Cover
1. **Shipping & Packaging:** How is the saree protected during transit? (Must convey care and premium unboxing without using forbidden words).
2. **Returns:** What is the return policy? (Must be transparent, clear, but firm on custom/bridal pieces).
3. **Care:** How should a handloom saree be cared for? (Must emphasize generational preservation, resting the fabric, and proper storage).
4. **Tailoring:** Do you offer blouse tailoring? (Highlight the complimentary first-fit service to reduce purchase friction).
5. **Provenance:** How do I know who wove my saree? (Reiterate the "weaver is named" brand promise).

## Knowledge Gaps
- None. Operational policies are standard, but the *delivery* of these policies must be elevated to match the brand voice.

## Editorial Constraints
- **Primary Emotion:** Confidence.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust.
- **Arc:** None (Question-Answer format).
- **No forbidden words:** Luxury, Premium, Exquisite, Perfect, Magic, Authentic.
