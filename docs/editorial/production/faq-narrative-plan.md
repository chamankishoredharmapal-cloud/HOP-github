# Narrative Plan: FAQ

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** None. Structured Q&A.
- **Tension Curve:** Flat. Focused purely on resolving questions and building confidence.

## Content Sequence
| Topic | Question | Key Editorial Message |
|-------|----------|------------------------|
| Shipping | "How is my saree prepared for shipping?" | Move beyond "we ship in a box." Describe the unbleached muslin, the jasmine paper, and the physical security of the shipment. |
| Returns | "What is your policy on returns?" | Clear 14-day window. Justify the final-sale nature of custom and bridal pieces through the lens of personalized labor. |
| Care | "How should I care for my handloom saree?" | Don't just say "dry clean." Explain the necessity of resting the fibers, avoiding direct sunlight, and refolding to prevent crease rot. |
| Tailoring | "Do you tailor the blouse piece?" | Frame complimentary first-fit tailoring as a commitment to how the garment lives on the body. |
| Provenance | "How do I know who wove my saree?" | Highlight the provenance card accompanying every delivery, naming the weaver and the month of completion. |

## Core Copy Directives
- **Remove:** Corporate speak (e.g., "We strive to provide excellent customer service").
- **Formatting:** React component update in `CustomerCare.tsx`. Use the existing Accordion UI structure.
