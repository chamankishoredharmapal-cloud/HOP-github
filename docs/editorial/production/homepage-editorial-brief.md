# Editorial Brief: Homepage

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Create immediate emotional connection and serve as a digital sanctuary that grounds the visitor in HOP's philosophy of Quiet Custodianship.
- **Business Goal:** Drive navigation to Collections (for buying intent) and Journal (for brand building intent).
- **Brand Goal:** Demonstrate Quiet Luxury through restraint in copy, lack of aggressive sales language, and a focus on time, craft, and lineage.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Wonder. The immediate feeling should be a pause, a breath of fresh air.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Quiet Confidence. HOP does not need to shout.
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Arrival.
- **Emotional Arc:** From the breathtaking visual of the hero → to the tactile reality of the craft → to the timelessness of the heirloom.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** To understand what HOP makes and what HOP believes.
- **Trust Requirement:** No "luxury" or "premium" labels. Value is proven through the mention of specific timeframes, human touch, and physical mechanics.

## Content Priorities
1. **SEO Metadata:** Clean, accurate, forbidden-word compliant.
2. **Hero Tagline:** A poetic distillation of the brand offering.
3. **Craft & Heirlooms Section:** Connect the object to the maker and the future wearer.
4. **Tone Registers:**
   - Hero: Sensory
   - Craft: Sensory/Technical
   - Heirlooms: Reflective
   - Collections: Trust
   - Journal: Resolution

## Production Scope
1. Draft the `homepage-copy.md` file containing all text for React integration.
2. Update React components (`Index.tsx`, `HeroSection.tsx`, etc.) with the new copy.
3. Remove any non-compliant placeholder copy currently in the components.
