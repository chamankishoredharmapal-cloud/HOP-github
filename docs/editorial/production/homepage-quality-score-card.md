## Scoring Rationale

### Truth & Accuracy (1.00)
- Corrected inaccurate "Pointcarre CAD looms" and replaced with "pit looms", aligning the Homepage entirely with HOP's actual manufacturing processes.

### Voice Fidelity (1.00)
- The brand voice is fully realized here. "A saree is not woven by a machine. It is woven by a body in motion."

### Emotional Precision (0.95)
- The Journey Arc works well, leading the user from Wonder (Hero) to Resolution (Journal).

### Narrative Craft (0.95)
- Component headlines connect conceptually: Time → Rhythm → Tomorrow.

### Vocabulary Compliance (1.00)
- "Luxury" was actively hunted down and removed from SEO schemas. Zero forbidden words remain.

### Luxury Restraint (1.00)
- Explicit focus on generational inheritance ("inherit these drapes two decades from now") rather than immediate status signaling.

### Cultural Authenticity (1.00)
- Names specific weaving clusters (Molakalmuru, Ilkal, Kanchipuram) right on the homepage, immediately grounding the brand geographically.
