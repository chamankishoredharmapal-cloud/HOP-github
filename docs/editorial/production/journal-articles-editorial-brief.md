# Editorial Brief: Journal Article (first 3-5)

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Establish HOP's authority in cultural aesthetics and material philosophy.
- **Business Goal:** Drive organic top-of-funnel traffic through high-quality editorial content that naturally links to products without hard-selling.
- **Brand Goal:** Position HOP as a cultural custodian that understands how women actually live and dress today.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Wonder (re-evaluating old assumptions about weddings and linen).
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust (in HOP's aesthetic judgment).
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Curiosity → Wonder.
- **Emotional Arc:** The reader begins with a conventional view (weddings must be loud, linen must be crisp) and ends with a refined, intentional view (weddings can be quiet, linen should yield).

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** Looking for aesthetic validation, cultural insight, or styling inspiration.
- **Trust Requirement:** Do not lecture. Observe and reflect. Use highly sensory, specific language.

## Content Priorities
1. **Transformation Arc:** Each article must pivot the reader's understanding.
2. **Integration:** Naturally embed references to the craft and products without making it sound like an advertisement.
