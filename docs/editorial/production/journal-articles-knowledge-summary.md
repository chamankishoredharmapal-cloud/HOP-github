## Articles to Produce

### 1. Five Drapes for a Quiet Wedding
- **Tag:** Occasion
- **Focus:** Re-framing the concept of wedding attire away from maximalism and toward intentionality. Suggesting sarees that hold their own through texture and drape rather than sequins and heavy gold.
- **Related Products:** Kalyani collection (Padmini), Viara collection (Viara Silk, Sakura).

### 2. Linen, and the Art of Doing Less
- **Tag:** Linen
- **Focus:** The philosophy of wearing linen. How its tendency to soften and crease is a feature of a life actually lived, not a flaw to be ironed out.
- **Related Products:** Megham Linen.

## Knowledge Gaps
- None. These articles are editorial syntheses of the brand philosophy established in Phase 2.

## Editorial Constraints
- **Primary Emotion:** Wonder.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust.
- **Arc:** Transformation Arc (Reader's understanding of the topic shifts).
- **No forbidden words:** Luxury, Exquisite, Perfect, Magic, Authentic.
