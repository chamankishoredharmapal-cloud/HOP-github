# Narrative Plan: Journal Article (first 3-5)

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Transformation Arc.
- **Tension Curve:** Observation (Hook) → Re-evaluation (Context) → Deep Dive (Depth) → Synthesis (Reflection).

## Scene Sequence (Five Drapes for a Quiet Wedding)
| Scene | Element | Purpose | Tone Register |
|-------|---------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Hook | Contrast the traditional big fat wedding with the rising desire for quiet, intimate ceremonies. | Reflective |
| 2 | Context | Why the clothing must change when the scale of the room changes. (You cannot wear a stadium outfit to a dinner table). | Analytical |
| 3 | Depth | Exploring specific drapes/fabrics that command attention through texture rather than shine (Silk, Organza). | Sensory |
| 4 | Reflection | The confidence required to dress quietly on a big day. | Reflective |

## Scene Sequence (Linen, and the Art of Doing Less)
| Scene | Element | Purpose | Tone Register |
|-------|---------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Hook | The modern obsession with crispness and control. | Analytical |
| 2 | Context | Introducing linen as the antidote to rigid perfection. | Reflective |
| 3 | Depth | The physical reality of handloom linen—how it responds to body heat and softens over hours. | Sensory |
| 4 | Reflection | Accepting the crease as a record of motion, not a failure of neatness. | Reflective |
