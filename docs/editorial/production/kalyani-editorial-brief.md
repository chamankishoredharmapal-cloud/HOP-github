# Editorial Brief: Kalyani Collection Detail Page

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose (one sentence):** Tell the story of the Kalyani collection, establishing its emotional context (winter weddings) and craft provenance (Molakalmuru temple border).
- **Business Goal:** Drive product discovery and create deep value justification before the user views the product grid.
- **Brand Goal:** Prove curatorial vision by naming the weaver (Gangamma), the geography (Molakalmuru), and the timeline (21 days) without falling into marketing clichés.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Focus (immersion into one specific narrative)
- **Secondary Emotion:** Confidence (trust built through transparency and specificity)
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Curiosity → Confidence
- **Emotional Arc:** Fascination with the mood (winter wedding) → Reverence for the craft (21 days, hand-spun zari) → Trust in the maker (Gangamma) → Confidence to explore the pieces.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well. Discriminating, reads long-form prose, looks for provenance, weave structure, and weaver attribution.
- **Reader Intent:** "What makes this collection special? Why should I invest in these sarees?"
- **Trust Requirement:** Radical transparency. Name the weaver, the village, the loom type, and the material. Avoid generic luxury terms.

## Content Priorities
1. **Mood:** Establish the specific feeling of Kalyani — not just "wedding" but "late afternoon winter ceremony."
2. **Craft:** Temple border, pit loom, 21 days, hand-spun zari.
3. **Weaver:** Gangamma (4th generation) and her relationship to the motif.
4. **Ritual:** The saree as a generational heirloom, stored carefully and passed down.

## Design Ratio (Coastal Blossom)
- **60% Space (Jasmine Mist):** Short paragraphs, unhurried pacing.
- **25% Depth (Coastal Teal):** Precise craft details (Molakalmuru, 12-momme silk).
- **10% Warmth (Golden Sand):** Gangamma's quote ("Some designs arrive complete").
- **5% Imperfection (Sakura Blush):** Mentioning the weather's effect on thread tension.

## Constraints
- **Forbidden Words:** Luxury, Premium, Exquisite, Perfect, Trend.
- **Tone Register:** Formality 3, Warmth 4 (intimate, personal), Sensory 4, Technical 2 (via System-2 block).
- **Format:** Frontmatter + markdown blocks (`hero`, `intro`, `body`, `image`, `system-2`, `closure`).
