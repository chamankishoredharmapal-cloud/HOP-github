# Narrative Plan: Kalyani Collection Detail Page

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Revelation — the reader discovers the depth of the collection layer by layer.
- **Tension Curve:** Initial visual immersion (mood) → Deepening respect (craft details) → Trust (human connection) → Resolution (heirloom perspective).

## Identity Transformation
- **From Identity:** A browser looking at "wedding sarees".
- **To Identity:** A custodian who understands the 21 days of labour, the 4 generations of skill, and the generational responsibility of owning a Kalyani drape.

## Resolution Design
- **Central Question Answered:** "Why is this collection special?" → Because it is woven over 21 days by a 4th generation weaver, capturing the exact light of a winter wedding.
- **Emotional Payoff:** The feeling of holding a piece of history.
- **Memory Anchor:** Gangamma's quote ("Some designs arrive complete") and the 21-day timeline.
