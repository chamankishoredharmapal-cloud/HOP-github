# Editorial Brief: Landing Pages (Atelier Appointments)

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Capture high-intent leads for bridal and custom styling by offering a private appointment.
- **Business Goal:** Increase direct high-AOV sales via personalized consultation.
- **Brand Goal:** Position the brand as a gracious host, moving away from aggressive e-commerce funnels to a relationship-based model.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Anticipation.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Confidence.
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Desire.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well (Brides, Collectors).
- **Reader Intent:** Wants personalized advice on drape, color, or custom weaving timelines.
- **Trust Requirement:** The form must feel like an invitation, not a data-harvesting tool.

## Content Priorities
1. **The Invitation:** Frame the appointment as "time spent together" rather than a "sales call."
2. **The Mediums:** Offer both "At the Atelier (Pondicherry)" and "A Quiet Call (Virtual)".
3. **Pacing:** Let the form breathe. Use calm, conversational labels (e.g., "How may we address you?" instead of "First Name").

## Production Scope
1. **Landing Page:** `src/pages/Appointments.tsx`
2. **Router Update:** `src/App.tsx`
3. **Header/Footer Update:** Link to appointments where appropriate.
