## Landing Page Concept
**Theme:** "The Private Viewing" (Atelier Appointments)
**Concept:** A dedicated landing page for high-intent customers (like brides or collectors) to request a private viewing or styling consultation at the Pondicherry atelier or virtually.

## Deliverables Required
1. **Landing Page Content:** React page at `src/pages/Appointments.tsx`.
2. **Copy:** Headline, subheadline, philosophy of the appointment, and form field labels.

## Knowledge Gaps
- None. We will build a standard lead capture layout but with the HOP editorial voice.

## Editorial Constraints
- **Primary Emotion:** Anticipation.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Confidence.
- **Arc:** Short-form Journey.
- **No forbidden words:** Luxury, Premium, Exquisite, Perfect, Magic, Authentic.
- **Tone:** Gracious host. Not "Book now," but "Request a viewing."
