# Editorial Brief: Lookbook

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Provide a purely aesthetic space for the customer to experience the visual weight and drape of the garments.
- **Business Goal:** Increase time on site and drive desire for high-ticket items.
- **Brand Goal:** Prove that the brand's visual language is as restrained and considered as its written language.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Desire.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Wonder.
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Desire → Confidence.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** Browsing for inspiration. "Show me the texture."
- **Trust Requirement:** High-quality imagery, not heavily photoshopped. The copy must not overshadow the visual.

## Content Priorities
1. **Silence:** The ratio of image to text should be 90/10. 
2. **Structural framing:** Organize the lookbook by physical aspects of the craft (e.g., The Loom, The Zari, The Drape).
3. **Captions:** Should read like museum placards, not sales pitches. (e.g., "Kalyani Brocade. Handwoven over forty days.")

## Production Scope
1. **Lookbook Page:** `src/pages/Lookbook.tsx`
2. **Router Update:** `src/App.tsx`
3. **Footer Update:** Link the lookbook under "The House" in `HopFooter.tsx`
