# Narrative Plan: Lookbook

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Visual Journey.
- **Tension Curve:** Steady state of wonder. The images draw the eye, while the minimal copy anchors the mind.

## Page Sequence (`src/pages/Lookbook.tsx`)

### 1. Hero
- **Visual:** Full bleed close-up of a border detail (Kalyani).
- **Headline:** The Visual Archive
- **Subtitle:** Studies in tension, thread, and time.

### 2. Section I: The Architecture of Silk
- **Framing Copy:** "A saree is a feat of engineering before it is an object of beauty. The structure of the weave determines how it holds light."
- **Visuals:** Grid of 3 images focusing on macro details (zari threads, borders, pallu).
- **Captions:**
  - "Kalyani Brocade. Hand-twisted silver zari."
  - "Viara Tissue. The warp and weft interacting."
  - "The heavy border of the Megham collection."

### 3. Section II: The Drape
- **Framing Copy:** "Silk only finds its true form when it meets gravity and the human body."
- **Visuals:** Asymmetrical layout showing the garments worn, full length and in motion.
- **Captions:**
  - "The unhurried fall of Kanchipuram silk."
  - "Linen holding its structure."

### 4. Closure
- **Visual:** A quiet, still life image of a folded saree in a box.
- **Call to Action:** "Explore the Collections"

## Core Copy Directives
- **Remove:** Hype, prices (prices belong on product pages), "Shop the look".
- **Formatting:** React page prioritizing large images, generous padding, and small serif captions.
