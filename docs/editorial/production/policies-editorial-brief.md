# Editorial Brief: Policies

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Translate standard legal and operational terms into the quiet, respectful voice of the house.
- **Business Goal:** Mitigate liability while reducing purchase anxiety.
- **Brand Goal:** Prove that HOP handles legalities with the same grace and restraint as its textiles.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Confidence.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust.
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Confidence (Post-evaluation).

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** Looking for the "catch." Checking return windows, shipping costs, and data handling.
- **Trust Requirement:** No obfuscation. No aggressive all-caps legal jargon where a quiet explanation will suffice.

## Content Priorities
1. **Plain Language:** Avoid heavy legalese where possible. Replace "User agrees to indemnify" with simpler boundaries.
2. **The "Why":** Ground operational rules in physical realities (e.g., custom pieces cannot be returned because they are cut for a specific body).

## Production Scope
1. Refine `src/pages/PrivacyPolicy.tsx`.
2. Refine `src/pages/TermsOfService.tsx`.
3. Create `src/pages/ShippingPolicy.tsx`.
4. Create `src/pages/ReturnsPolicy.tsx`.
5. Update `src/App.tsx` and `src/components/hop/HopFooter.tsx` to link to all four policies.
