# Narrative Plan: Policies

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Document Structure.
- **Tension Curve:** Flat. Authoritative and reassuring.

## Document Sequences

### 1. Privacy Policy
- **Hook:** "Quietly kept."
- **Body:** What we collect (minimum necessary), how we use it (fulfillment, not marketing algorithms), your rights (transparency).
- **Tone:** Respectful boundary setting.

### 2. Terms of Service
- **Hook:** "A small house, a simple agreement."
- **Body:** Acceptance of variance in handloom, respectful use of IP, quiet jurisdiction.
- **Tone:** Firm but gracious.

### 3. Shipping Policy
- **Hook:** "From the loom to your hands."
- **Body:** Packaging process (muslin, jasmine), dispatch timelines, fully insured worldwide transit, duties and taxes.
- **Tone:** Meticulous and caring.

### 4. Returns Policy
- **Hook:** "Time to decide."
- **Body:** 14-day window. Process for initiating a return. Justification for final sale items (custom tailoring, bespoke bridal).
- **Tone:** Fair and logical.

## Core Copy Directives
- **Remove:** All-caps shouting, generic legal templates.
- **Formatting:** React pages using `PageLayout` and semantic HTML sections.
