# Editorial Brief: Product Detail (First 3)

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Tell the story of individual sarees, creating desire through specificity (sensory details) and building trust through transparency (craft specs).
- **Business Goal:** Convert collection browsing into product-level desire and purchase consideration.
- **Brand Goal:** Prove that every saree in the house has a story worth its price. Show the difference between mass-market cataloging and digital fashion house storytelling.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Desire (sensory immersion).
- **Secondary Emotion:** Confidence (System-2 validation).
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Trust → Desire → Confidence.
- **Emotional Arc:** Sensory capture (hero/intro) → Identity reflection (who wears this) → Technical validation (specs) → Decision (closure).

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well. She is not buying impulsively. She is looking for a reason why *this* specific drape belongs to her.
- **Reader Intent:** "How does it feel? Where is it from? Will it suit my life?"
- **Trust Requirement:** Provide weight, texture, and origin without hyperbole. 

## Content Priorities
1. **Sensory Story:** The way the fabric moves, catches light, or feels against the skin.
2. **Context:** Where the saree is worn (e.g., reception, daily layered life).
3. **Transparency:** System-2 blocks must be precise and unembellished.

## Design Ratio (Coastal Blossom)
- **60% Space:** Unhurried pacing, clear distinct blocks.
- **25% Depth:** Detailed system-2 block, specific fabric mentions (e.g., 450g pure linen).
- **10% Warmth:** Mention of the wearer's life or the weaver's intention.
- **5% Imperfection:** Details like the subtle shift in colour or the organic texture of handwoven linen.

## Constraints
- **Forbidden Words:** Luxury, Premium, Exquisite, Perfect, Wardrobe Essential, Statement Piece.
- **Tone Register:** Formality 3, Warmth 4, Sensory 5, Technical 2, Urgency 0.
- **Format:** Frontmatter (UUID, tagline, sensoryStory) + markdown blocks (hero, intro, body, pull-quote, system-2, closure).
