# Narrative Plan: Product Detail (First 3)

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Revelation — details are revealed progressively to build value perception.
- **Tension Curve:** Sensory Hook (texture/light) → Identity Connection (who wears this) → Grounding (specs) → Resolution (the choice).

## Scene Sequence (Applies to all products)
| Scene | Element | Purpose | Emotion | Tone Register |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Hero & Frontmatter | Establish visual and sensory baseline before scrolling. | Desire | Sensory |
| 2 | Intro Block | The emotional hook of the drape. | Fascination | Reflective |
| 3 | Body 1 (Sensory) | Texture, movement, weight, light response. | Desire | Sensory |
| 4 | Body 2 (Context) | Where it is worn, the woman who wears it. | Belonging | Warmth |
| 5 | Pull-Quote | A memorable anchor phrase. | Focus | Reflective |
| 6 | System-2 Block | Technical validation (fabric, origin, care). | Confidence | Technical |
| 7 | Closure Block | Final thought on the lifecycle of the saree. | Trust | Resolution |
