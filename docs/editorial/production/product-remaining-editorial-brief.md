# Editorial Brief: Product Detail (remaining) - Sakura

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Translate the physical reality of a 350-gram organza saree with hand-embroidery into an emotional proposition.
- **Business Goal:** Overcome the barrier of a 14-day dispatch time and a higher price point (22,500) by framing the wait and the cost as functions of meticulous craft.
- **Brand Goal:** Prove that an Ivory Organza saree is not fragile, but deliberate.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Desire (the sensory appeal of the sheer fabric and embroidery).
- **Secondary Emotion:** Confidence (trust in the construction and quality).
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Desire → Confidence.
- **Emotional Arc:** The reader sees the visual lightness → feels the textural weight of the embroidery → understands the time it takes to make → accepts the 14-day wait as proof of value.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** "Why does this organza take 14 days? Will it hold its shape?"
- **Trust Requirement:** Be clear about the weight (350g) and the care required. Do not over-promise on durability; respect the nature of organza.

## Content Priorities
1. **Sensory Introduction:** The contrast between the sheer organza and the dense embroidery.
2. **Craft Details:** The hand-embroidery process that necessitates the 14-day dispatch.
3. **Identity:** Framing Ivory as a choice of presence, not absence.
