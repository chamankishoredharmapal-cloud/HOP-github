# Editorial Brief: SEO Metadata Pass

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Ensure all pages have high-quality metadata for search engines and social sharing, without resorting to cheap e-commerce SEO tactics.
- **Business Goal:** Improve organic discovery and brand perception on SERPs.
- **Brand Goal:** Every snippet must sound like House of Padmavati.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Trust.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Curiosity.

## Content Priorities
1. **Title Tags:** `[Page Name] · House of Padmavati`. Clean and predictable.
2. **Meta Descriptions:** 150-160 characters. Must describe the page accurately while using the brand's poetic vocabulary (e.g., "Join the atelier to manage your orders" instead of "Sign up for an account today to get discounts").

## Production Scope
1. Audit `src/pages`.
2. Add `useMetadata` to missing public-facing pages (`CustomerCare.tsx`, `Signup.tsx`).
