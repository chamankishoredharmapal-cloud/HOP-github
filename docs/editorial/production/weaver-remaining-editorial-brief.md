# Editorial Brief: Weaver Story (remaining)

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Tell the human story behind the handloom linen and organza embroidery.
- **Business Goal:** Build human connection to justify the cost and timeline of non-silk and craft-heavy products. Fulfill the brand promise: "The weaver is named."
- **Brand Goal:** Ground the product in human hands, showing respect for the artisan's time and generational knowledge.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Connection.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust.
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Trust → Belonging.
- **Emotional Arc:** From observing the artisan's technique to understanding their personal discipline and lineage.

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well.
- **Reader Intent:** "Who made this saree? What is their life like?"
- **Trust Requirement:** Treat the artisans with profound respect. Avoid pity or romanticizing poverty. Focus on their mastery, agency, and skill.

## Content Priorities
1. **Sensory Detail:** The sound of the loom in Phulia; the silence of the embroidery frame in Lucknow.
2. **Lineage:** Connect their current work to their ancestors' teachings.
3. **The Work:** Tie Saraswati directly to the Megham linen saree, and Rafeeq to the Sakura organza saree.
