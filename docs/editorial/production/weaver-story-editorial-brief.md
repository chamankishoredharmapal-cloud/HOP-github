# Editorial Brief: Weaver Story (First 2)

## Strategic Mandate
- **Purpose:** Fulfill the brand promise: "The weaver is named." Build human connection through specificity rather than sentimentality.
- **Business Goal:** Differentiate HOP from quiet luxury brands that hide their supply chain. Establish cultural custodianship.
- **Brand Goal:** Position weavers as masters of their craft, not objects of charity.

## Emotional Targeting
- **Primary Emotion:** Connection.
- **Secondary Emotion:** Trust.
- **Customer Journey Stage:** Trust → Belonging.
- **Emotional Arc:** The reader sees the weaver at work (First sight) → understands her skill (Her hands) → recognizes her place in history (Lineage).

## Reader
- **Primary Persona:** The Woman Who Chooses Well. She values provenance and wants to know who made her clothing.
- **Reader Intent:** "Who wove this saree?"
- **Trust Requirement:** Provide exact names, locations, and timelines (e.g., 43 years). Do not invent emotional struggles; state the facts of the labor.

## Content Priorities
1. **Portrait Arc:** Progression from visual observation to deep technical appreciation.
2. **Technical Mastery:** Mention specific techniques (e.g., Tope Teni in Ilkal).
3. **Restraint:** Let the duration of the labor (31 years, 43 years) speak for itself without adding adjectives like "incredible" or "magical."
