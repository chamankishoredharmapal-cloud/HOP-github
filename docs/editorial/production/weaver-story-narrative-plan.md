# Narrative Plan: Weaver Story (First 2)

## Arc
- **Arc Type:** Portrait Arc.
- **Tension Curve:** Observation (First sight) → Technical Detail (Hands) → History (Lineage) → Culmination (The work).

## Scene Sequence
| Scene | Element | Purpose | Emotion | Tone Register |
|-------|---------|---------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Hero & Intro | Introduce the weaver by name and establish her tenure. | Connection | Reflective |
| 2 | Body 1 (Hands/Technique) | Describe the physical reality of her specific technique. | Focus | Technical |
| 3 | Image Block | Close-up of hands at work. | Trust | — |
| 4 | Body 2 (Lineage/Time) | Explain how she learned and the repetitive discipline involved. | Respect | Warmth/Reflective |
| 5 | Pull-Quote | Her own words on the craft. | Connection | Reflective |
| 6 | Closure | A concluding thought on her contribution to the wearer's life. | Belonging | Resolution |

## Individual Story Focus

### 1. Gangamma of Molakalmuru
- **Hook:** 31 years of weaving the same border pattern.
- **Mechanics:** Adjusting thread tension twice an hour; pit loom mechanics.
- **Human Element:** Learning from her grandmother.
- **Outcome:** The saree carries what it needs to carry.

### 2. Lakshmidevamma of Ilkal
- **Hook:** 43 years of the Tope Teni border.
- **Mechanics:** The physical interlocking of the border with the body — a technique requiring a second pair of hands or extreme dexterity.
- **Human Element:** The muscle memory developed over four decades.
- **Outcome:** She weaves not for perfection, but for the rhythm.
