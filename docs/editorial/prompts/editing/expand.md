# Editing: Expand

**Purpose:** Elaborate on existing HOP content — add sensory detail, contextual depth, or narrative development — without padding or filler.

**When to use:** A piece of content is too brief for its purpose. It needs more sensory description, more craft context, or more narrative development. Not every piece needs expanding — only use this when the content is genuinely thin.

**When NOT to use:** For condensing content (use `editing/shorten.md`). For rewriting off-brand content (use `editing/rewrite.md`). Do not expand content that is already dense enough.

---

## Purpose

Add depth to existing HOP content by elaborating on what is already there. The expansion must feel inevitable — as if the missing sentences were always meant to be there.

## Required Inputs

- The content to expand
- The specific area that needs expansion: sensory description, craft context, human detail, emotional resonance, or structural development
- Any additional factual information that can be added (from research or context)

## Expected Outputs

- Expanded content with new sentences or paragraphs inserted
- A note explaining what was added and why

## Constraints

- Do not add padding. Every added sentence must serve a purpose.
- Do not repeat what the original already says. Expand, not reiterate.
- Do not add marketing language or generic descriptors.
- Do not add facts unless you have verified sources.
- Preserve the original's structure and flow. Insert new content where it naturally belongs.

## Expansion Targets

| Thin Content | Add |
|--------------|-----|
| "The zari border is beautiful." | Sensory description of how the zari catches light, how it feels, how it behaves over time. |
| "She wore it to a wedding." | Context: whose wedding, when, what kind of ceremony, how it carried her through the day. |
| "This weave takes twenty-one days." | Why: because the warp must be set, the border woven first, the zari spun by hand. |
| "Handwoven in Karnataka." | Specific village, weaver name, weaving tradition, generational context. |
| "This saree is made of silk." | What kind of silk, what weight, how it behaves differently from other silks. |

## Examples

**Original:** "This saree is handwoven in pure Mulberry silk with a temple border."

**Expanded:** "This saree is woven from pure Mulberry silk, 12-momme, on a pit loom in Molakalmuru. The temple border is Gangamma's signature — a pattern she learned from her grandmother, unchanged in three generations. The border is woven first, then the body, then the pallu. Each section requires a separate setup."

**What was added:** Weave location, weaver name, generational context, technical detail about the weaving process. No padding — every sentence adds information.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Adding generic descriptions | "The saree is really beautiful" — replace with specific sensory detail or remove. |
| Repeating the original | If the new sentence says the same thing as the existing one, remove it. |
| Breaking the rhythm | Maintain HOP's sentence rhythm pattern (short → medium → long → short). |
| Adding filler transitions | No "however," "moreover," "in addition." If the new content belongs, it follows naturally. |
