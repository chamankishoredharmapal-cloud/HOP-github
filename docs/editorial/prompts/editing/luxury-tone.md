# Editing: Luxury Tone

**Purpose:** Refine existing content for the luxury register — increase sensory density, improve specificity, and eliminate any tone that undermines HOP's quiet luxury positioning.

**When to use:** Content that is factually correct and structurally sound but does not yet feel luxurious enough. The words are right but the texture is wrong.

**When NOT to use:** For content that needs a complete rewrite (use `editing/rewrite.md`). For content that has factual errors (use `review/fact-checking.md` first). If the content already feels luxurious, do not over-refine.

---

## Purpose

Elevate the sensory and emotional texture of HOP content. The goal is not to add "luxury words" (which are forbidden) but to increase specificity, sensory density, and restraint.

## Required Inputs

- The content to refine
- The target luxury indicators to improve: sensory density, specificity, restraint, or generational framing

## Expected Outputs

- Refined content with improved luxury texture
- A note on which luxury indicators were improved

## Constraints

- Never add the word "luxury," "premium," "exquisite," "opulent," or any forbidden luxury cliché
- Never decrease clarity for the sake of tone
- Never add claims that are not supported by facts
- Preserve all factual information

## Luxury Indicators to Improve

### 1. Sensory Density
Replace abstract descriptions with sensory specifics.

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "A beautiful silk saree" | "A Mulberry silk that reads as teal in shadow and jade in sunlight." |
| "Soft and comfortable fabric" | "The fabric settles at the hip with a weight that says 'I am here.'" |

### 2. Specificity
Replace generic references with specific details.

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "Handwoven by skilled artisans" | "Woven by Gangamma in Molakalmuru, on a pit loom her grandmother used." |
| "Premium quality zari" | "Hand-spun zari, real silver with gold wash." |

### 3. Restraint
Remove words that weaken the impact.

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "This saree is really very special and unique." | "This saree is one of twelve." |
| "An absolutely stunning piece for any occasion." | "This drape belongs to winter ceremonies." |

### 4. Generational Framing
Frame objects in terms of time and legacy.

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| "A high-quality saree that will last." | "A saree made to outlive its first wearer." |
| "Durable zari that won't tarnish." | "Real zari that softens with age — the opposite of imitation." |

## Examples

**Before (adequate but not luxurious):**
"This silk saree is handwoven with gold zari. It is suitable for weddings. The border has a traditional design."

**After (luxury-refined):**
"Pure Mulberry silk, 12-momme, handwoven on a pit loom in Molakalmuru. The zari is real silver with gold wash — hand-spun, flexible, glowing. The temple border carries a pattern that Gangamma learned from her grandmother. This drape belongs to winter weddings where the light fades by five."

**What changed:**
- "silk saree" → "Pure Mulberry silk, 12-momme" (specificity)
- "handwoven" → "handwoven on a pit loom in Molakalmuru" (specificity)
- "gold zari" → "real silver with gold wash — hand-spun, flexible, glowing" (sensory + specificity)
- "suitable for weddings" → "belongs to winter weddings where the light fades by five" (specific + sensory)
- "traditional design" → "temple border, Gangamma's grandmother's pattern" (specific + generational)

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Adding forbidden words | "Luxurious fabric" — never. Replace with specific sensory description. |
| Over-writing | More words do not equal more luxury. Restraint is a luxury signal. |
| Making it cold | Luxury refinement should increase warmth, not decrease it. |
| Losing clarity | If a sentence becomes harder to understand, the refinement has failed. |
