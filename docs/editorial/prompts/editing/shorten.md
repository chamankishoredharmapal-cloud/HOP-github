# Editing: Shorten

**Purpose:** Condense existing HOP content without losing its essence, sensory quality, or emotional resonance.

**When to use:** Content is too long for its purpose. A product description needs to fit a tighter layout. A journal article runs long. A collection narrative has redundant passages.

**When NOT to use:** For expanding content (use `editing/expand.md`). For rewriting off-brand content (use `editing/rewrite.md`). If the content is already concise, do not shorten it.

---

## Purpose

Reduce word count while preserving meaning, sensory density, and emotional impact. The condensed version should feel tighter, not thinner.

## Required Inputs

- The content to shorten
- Target length (optional — if no target is given, remove all unnecessary words)

## Expected Outputs

- Shortened content
- A summary of what was removed and why

## Constraints

- Preserve all factual claims. Shortening removes words, not facts.
- Preserve sensory density. A shortened piece must still have sensory anchors.
- Preserve emotional resonance. The last line must still land.
- Do not remove the weaver's name, the village, or the technique.
- Do not convert HOP's warm voice into a cold, clipped tone.

## What to Cut First

| Cut Priority | Examples |
|-------------|----------|
| 1. Empty intensifiers | absolutely, truly, really, very, extremely, incredibly |
| 2. Redundant adjectives | "beautiful" when the description already conveys beauty |
| 3. Generic praise | stunning, gorgeous, amazing, wonderful |
| 4. Transition words | however, moreover, furthermore, nevertheless (HOP does not use these) |
| 5. Marketing phrases | "we believe," "our mission," "the HOP difference" |
| 6. Explanations of what the reader already knows | If the reader can infer it, remove the explanation |
| 7. Second statements of the same idea | If two sentences say the same thing, keep the better one |
| 8. Unnecessary prepositional phrases | "the border of the saree" → "the border" |

## Examples

**Original:** "This saree is truly a stunning example of the weaver's incredible skill and artistry. The beautiful zari border catches the morning light in an absolutely breathtaking way."

**Shortened:** "The zari border catches the morning light — not the harsh noon light, but the soft gold of early day."

**What was removed:** "truly," "stunning example," "incredible skill," "artistry," "beautiful," "absolutely breathtaking." All empty intensifiers and generic praise. The sensory description conveys the quality without claiming it.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Losing sensory density | After shortening, check that sensory anchors remain. If all sensory language was cut, add one back. |
| Becoming cold | HOP's voice is warm. Shortening should not produce a clipped, robotic tone. Read the result aloud. |
| Cutting specificity | Keep the weaver name, village, technique, brand colour names. These are HOP's identifiers. |
| Breaking rhythm | After cuts, verify sentence rhythm still follows HOP's pattern. |
