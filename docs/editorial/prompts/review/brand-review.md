# Review: Brand Review

**Purpose:** Audit content for brand voice fidelity, identity alignment, and vocabulary compliance.

**When to use:** Before publication of any content. After writing or editing, as a check that the content accurately represents the HOP brand. Also used for periodic brand consistency audits of published content.

**When NOT to use:** For comprehensive quality review (use `review/quality-checklist.md`). For factual accuracy (use `review/fact-checking.md`). Brand review is specifically about identity and voice.

---

## Purpose

Ensure every piece of HOP content accurately represents the brand — its identity, voice, vocabulary, messaging pillars, and emotional positioning.

## Required Inputs

- The content to review
- The page or touchpoint the content is intended for

## Expected Outputs

- Brand fidelity score (percentage)
- List of brand violations with severity and recommendations
- Overall assessment: On-Brand / Near-Brand / Off-Brand

## Brand Review Criteria

### 1. Identity Alignment

Does the content reflect HOP's brand territories?

| Territory | How It Should Appear |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Quiet Luxury | No claimed luxury, no logos, no visible branding in text |
| Cultural Custodianship | Specific cultural references, named weavers, named regions |
| Slow Making | Time references, process descriptions, human effort |
| Human Scale | Named people, specific places, individual stories |
| Generational Thinking | Legacy framing, objects that outlive owners |

**Violation:** Content that positions HOP as a marketplace, fast-fashion, or discount destination.

### 2. Voice Fidelity

Does the content match HOP's voice dimensions?

| Dimension | Acceptable Range |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Formality | 2-4/5 |
| Warmth | 3-5/5 |
| Sensory Density | 3-5/5 |
| Technical Depth | 1-3/5 |
| Urgency | 0-1/5 |
| Imperative Force | 1-2/5 |

**Violation:** Content that sounds corporate, cold, salesy, or urgent.

### 3. Messaging Pillar Alignment

Does the content serve the correct messaging pillar for its page?

Check the expected primary pillar from `docs/editorial/02-brand-architecture/messaging-pillars.md`. If the content serves a different pillar, assess whether the mismatch is intentional or accidental.

**Violation:** Content that undermines its page's primary pillar (e.g., urgency language on a Quietness page).

### 4. Vocabulary Compliance

Does the content use only approved vocabulary and avoid all forbidden words?

**Violation:** Any forbidden word. Any use of "customer," "consumer," "buyer," or "user." Any generic colour name not in the brand palette.

### 5. Human-Scale Test

Is the content written for one woman, not a demographic?

**Pass:** Uses "you" and "she," includes human details, names specific people.

**Fail:** Uses "the customer," "our buyers," "the modern woman" (archetype, not person).

### 6. Brand Promise Alignment

Does the content align with HOP's brand promises?

| Promise | What to Check |
|---------|--------------|
| No discounts ever | Content does not mention sales, discounts, or coupons |
| No urgency ever | No "limited time," "hurry," "only X left" |
| No marketing noise | No pop-up language, no aggressive CTAs |
| The weaver is named | If a product is mentioned, the weaver is named or attributed |
| Wrapped with intention | If packaging is mentioned, it is described specifically |

## Scoring

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **On-Brand (90-100%)** | Content accurately represents HOP brand. Minor improvements optional. |
| **Near-Brand (70-89%)** | Content is generally on-brand but has several issues that should be fixed before publication. |
| **Off-Brand (<70%)** | Content does not represent HOP. Requires rewrite before publication. |

## Finding Format

```
### Violation {N}: {Type}

**Severity:** critical | important | minor
**Criterion:** {which brand criterion is violated}
**Location:** {paragraph, sentence}
**Issue:** {what is wrong}
**Recommendation:** {specific fix}
**Brand impact:** {how this violation affects brand perception}
```
