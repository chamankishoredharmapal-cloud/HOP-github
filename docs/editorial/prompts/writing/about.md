# Writing: About

**Purpose:** Generate the About (Our Story) page content — the brand's origin story, philosophy, and invitation.

**When to use:** Creating or refreshing the About page.

**When NOT to use:** For individual content types within the editorial system.

---

## Objective

Tell the HOP origin story without sounding like a founding myth. The About page must feel like a conversation with a friend, not a press release. It serves the Trust and Belonging emotional stages.

## Audience

A woman who has browsed the collections and wants to know who is behind them. She is deciding whether to trust this brand. She is reading with scrutiny.

## Tone

| Dimension | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Formality | 3/5 |
| Warmth | 4/5 |
| Sensory Density | 4/5 |
| Technical Depth | 1/5 |
| Urgency | 0/5 |
| Imperative Force | 1/5 |

**Reading level:** Grade 8

## Structure

### The Story
- Who is Padmavati? A woman, not a muse. Her name is a dedication.
- How the house came to be — through observation, not business planning.
- What the house stands for — shown through action, not stated as values.

### The Philosophy
- The Coastal Blossom Philosophy briefly explained
- The five brand territories as they manifest in real decisions

### The Invitation
- What the reader can expect from HOP
- An open door, not a CTA

## Required Context

- The brand's origin narrative
- Key brand territories and messaging pillars
- Any founding stories involving Padmavati

## Required References

- `docs/editorial/02-brand-architecture/brand-bible.md`
- `docs/editorial/02-brand-architecture/messaging-pillars.md`
- `docs/editorial/07-emotional-architecture/emotional-journey.md` (Trust, Belonging stages)

## Formatting

- The word "luxury" must not appear
- The phrase "we believe" must not appear
- The word "mission" must not appear
- Sensory density: 4/5
- No exclamation marks, no questions directed at the reader

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The story of Padmavati is told as a dedication, not a branding exercise
- [ ] Brand territories are shown through examples, not listed as bullet points
- [ ] No "we believe," "our mission," "our vision"
- [ ] No claims about being "luxury" or "premium"
- [ ] The invitation is warm but not transactional
- [ ] Zero forbidden words

## Example Output

The About page should read like:

> Padmavati is not a fictional muse. She is the woman whose hands and life informed this house's understanding of what a saree can mean. The name is a dedication — not a brand invention, but an acknowledgment of a debt. A saree, she taught us, is never just cloth. It is a vessel for memory, a carrier of lineage, a thing that outlives its first wearer.

## Failure Cases

| Failure | Symptom | Correction |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Corporate origin story | "House of Padmavati was founded in 2024 with a mission to..." | Replace with human narrative: "It began with a woman named Padmavati and a question about what a saree can mean." |
| Value signalling | "We believe in authenticity, craftsmanship, and sustainability." | Show these through specific stories, not abstract claims. |
| Luxury framing | "We are a luxury handloom brand." | Remove. Let the specificity signal the quality. |
| Too long | More than 500 words of narrative | Condense. Every sentence must earn its place. |
