# Writing: Homepage

**Purpose:** Generate hero taglines, section copy, and subtitle copy for the HOP homepage.

**When to use:** Creating or refreshing homepage content — hero section, collection stage, craft section, journal preview, newsletter invitation.

**When NOT to use:** For individual product or collection pages (use `writing/product-pages.md` or `writing/collections.md`). For SEO metadata on the homepage (use `writing/seo-copy.md`).

---

## Objective

Create copy that makes the visitor feel they have entered a quiet room. The homepage is the Arrival and Curiosity stages of the emotional journey. It must signal that HOP is not an ecommerce store without saying so directly.

## Audience

First-time visitors who have never heard of HOP. They do not yet know what HOP is. The homepage must teach them — implicitly, through sensory experience, not through explicit statements.

## Tone

| Dimension | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Formality | 3/5 |
| Warmth | 4/5 |
| Sensory Density | 5/5 |
| Technical Depth | 1/5 |
| Urgency | 0/5 |
| Imperative Force | 1/5 |

**Reading level:** Grade 8

## Structure

### Hero Section
- No headline in the traditional sense. A short, poetic line that sets the atmosphere.
- No CTA. The only action is scrolling.
- The tagline should feel like an invitation, not a statement.

### Collection Stage
- Collection names presented as women's names (Kalyani, Viara, etc.)
- Taglines hint but do not explain fully
- Each collection gets one line of atmosphere

### Craft Section
- One powerful craft claim: "Sixty hours per drape. No shortcuts."
- Sensory description of the making process
- Naming a weaver, a village, a technique

### Journal Preview
- Article titles and deks
- One line per article

### Newsletter Invitation
- "Follow the journal" rather than "subscribe"
- No urgency, no incentive, no pressure

## Required Context

- The current collection names and taglines (from collections database)
- The current featured journal articles (from content service)
- Any seasonal or campaign context

## Formatting

- Hero: 1-2 lines, no punctuation at end
- Collection taglines: short, atmospheric, ≤120 characters
- Craft section: short declarative sentence, followed by 2-3 sensory sentences
- Journal preview: title + dek pairs
- Newsletter: one sentence, warm, no pressure

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Hero tagline does not explain what HOP is — it creates an atmosphere
- [ ] No exclamation marks, no questions, no imperatives
- [ ] Collection taglines hint but do not explain
- [ ] Craft section includes a specific number or weaver detail
- [ ] Newsletter invitation uses "follow the journal" not "subscribe"
- [ ] Zero forbidden words
- [ ] No explicit statements of brand philosophy ("we believe," "our mission")

## Example Output

**Hero:**
> Where cloth carries memory.

**Collection tagline (Kalyani):**
> Wedding Elegance · Heritage Luxury

**Craft section:**
> Sixty hours per drape. No shortcuts. Hand-spun zari on a pit loom in Molakalmuru, where Gangamma has been weaving the same temple border for thirty-one years. The thread remembers.

## Failure Cases

| Failure | Symptom | Correction |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Generic luxury homepage | "Experience luxury handloom" | Remove all luxury labels. Replace with specific sensory details. |
| Explaining the brand | "House of Padmavati is a digital fashion house for..." | Remove. Let the experience explain the brand. |
| Pressure CTA | "Shop the collection" as hero CTA | Remove hero CTA. The only action is scrolling. |
| Marketing language | "Discover your perfect saree" | Replace with atmospheric, sensory language. |
